Development diary: TerraWeave

Every stage of TerraWeave with screenshots, what was verified and what was not yet.

  1. Nov 22, 2026
    Donemain

    TerraWeave: rock textures without a swirl at the top

    Generated TerraWeave rocks were unwrapped like a globe, and at the top the texture twisted into a swirl. Each face now takes the projection of its main axis, so the rock is covered with the same texture density on every side.

    Rocks are built from a sphere cut by planes and deformed by the seed. Texture coordinates used to come from that sphere's latitude and longitude: at the poles all meridians meet in one point and the rock texture stretched into a spiral at the top.

    Each triangle now gets a planar projection along the axis it faces most, in metres with the texture repeat. Faces already have their own vertices, so switching the projection adds no seams to the mesh.

    The generator test checks that the unwrap area per surface area stays within one projection for every triangle of every LOD - no pinched poles.

    Verified
    Mac (Metal), aiverse_modelview: rounded, boulder and shard rocks with seed 42 from above and the side, dark-rock 1024² textures; TerraWeave tests 4 of 4.
    Limitations
    The terraweave-rocks package in the catalog still has the old unwrap: it needs a re-export and a new version. Where projections meet the texture can turn slightly.

  2. Nov 21, 2026
    In progressagent-wind-masks · 18f16ca16

    TERRA ground without tiling or blotches

    Up close the ground samples grass, sand and rock from shared world-space detail layers instead of the baked tile texture; a second rotated lookup and macro noise break the repeat.

    Previously a 64 m tile was baked into a 1024 px texture (about 6 cm per pixel) with top-down grass, which stretched into blotches on slopes. Now the tile texture only stores layer weights (bilinear) and the 4 m detail layers use dominant-axis projection, so slopes do not stretch.

    Against tiling, grass and sand get a second lookup rotated ~53° and rescaled, mixed by world gradient noise, plus a two-octave brightness variation (23 m and 7 m). The graph stays within 16 samples; material graphs gain a nodeBudget of up to 192 nodes.

    Verified
    Mac (Metal), AlpineValley review copy: close-up bank frames; shader_graph test (nodeBudget).
    Limitations
    Enabled on the review copy; the main AlpineValley project still uses baked textures.
    Native landscape authoring
  3. Nov 21, 2026
    In progressagent-wind-masks · c304a51cd

    TerraWeave forest: a whole tree per draw, impostors in the distance

    Identical trees now render as one instanced draw per detail level instead of one draw per branch, and distant trees use impostors baked from the near model.

    With hierarchical wind a tree becomes one model with three tiers (full, reduced, minimal); FloraView picks the tier and Renderer3D instances identical trees per primitive. A forest frame went from ~6000 draws to 110–177.

    The impostor atlas is baked by rasterising the near model with 2×2 supersampling and colour bleeding, so a distant tree keeps its silhouette and crown colour.

    Verified
    Mac (Metal), editor: same-scene draw call and GPU time measurements before/after; tests wind_rig, terraweave_model, terraweave_forest_scene.
    Limitations
    Static glTF trees from older exports (as in AlpineValley) still have no LOD chains; horizon-wide impostor grouping is still being verified.
