Meta and LiveOps

Shop, quests and events from JSON

A ready meta layer for mobile games: currencies, shop, passes, daily rewards, clans, tournaments and events.

Development article

What is inside

Economy

Currencies, items, shop, crafting, gifts and real-money purchases.

Progression

Quests, achievements, passes, daily login, energy, mail.

Social

Leaderboards, chats with moderation, friends and clans.

LiveOps

Schedules, events, tournaments, chests with pity, referrals, rewarded ads.

Retries without double rewards

Operations carry ids: a lost reply never grants a reward twice.

What it lets you do

  • Assemble the meta of a mobile game from ready blocks.
  • Run scheduled events without a client update.
  • Connect your own backend to ready logic.
