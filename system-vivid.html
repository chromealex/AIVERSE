Digital humans

Digital humans: a facial controller and GPU morphs on top of MotionWeave animation, the Character Creator appearance editor in Studio and an adapter for MetaHuman exports.

in development since Nov 19, 2026

Development articleDevelopment diary · 2

What is inside

Facial controller

Semantic facial channels, visemes, expressions and gaze on top of the MotionWeave pose.

GPU morphs

Sparse morphs through compute shaders.

Skin and LOD

Skin LOD transfer, separate materials for skin, eyes and hair.

Hair

Groom collisions with a reusable buffer.

Attachments

Named sockets and independent skinned models.

Character Creator

An editor in Studio: per-part face blending, brush sculpting, skin, makeup, eyes, hair, body, outfit and hairstyle try-on, expressions and light, presets, crowds, LOD build and “To the game”.

MetaHuman adapter

MetaHuman exports with no Unreal at runtime: material roles, face meshes, ARKit coverage, corrective joint, jaw and eye drivers.

What it lets you do

  • Build digital humans: cutscenes, NPCs, avatars, dialogue portraits.
  • Bring faces to life with expressions, blinking and gaze on top of any body animation.
  • Show characters in close-up without stepped shadows.
  • Build characters in the editor without a DCC: from a preset to a unit in the game in minutes.
  • Take ready MetaHuman characters and adjust their look in Studio right away.
