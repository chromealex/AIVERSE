    NEREID: water turbidity above and below the surface

    Water gains a turbidity parameter (with a silt colour): one value changes both the look from above and the visibility when diving.

    resolvedWaterOptics() turns turbidity into shared extinction, a milky silt colour and a scattering share; both the surface shader and PRISMA's underwater view read them. From above the water uses the real optical path — light down to the bed plus the slanted view ray — so turbid water also looks turbid from outside.

    At 0 the water looks as before. The parameter lives in the surface style, the NereidWater component and cascades, is validated and is part of cache keys.

    Verified
    Mac (Metal), AlpineValley review copy: identical frames above and under water at 0 / 0.25 / 0.6; ecs_nereid_renderer_contract test.
