Engine systems / LENS

LENS

since 3.0.0

Cameras and cinematic frames

Camera scenes as data: several cameras, frame scheduling, image capture and authoring through MCP and Studio.

in development since Nov 20, 2026

System docsDevelopment diary · 5

What is inside

Cameras in ECS

Camera assets, frame scheduling and dependency checks.

Render targets

Managed colour and depth targets for every camera.

Rigs and history

Camera rigs and separate temporal state for every camera.

Behaviours and presets

Camera bodies for any game — map overview, follow, chase, first person, planet orbit — and ready presets in one line of data.

Stack and director

Overlays, split-screen, picture-in-picture and a director that blends between cameras.

Lens and post-processing

Per-camera post-processing, exposure and depth of field; layer masks.

Capture

GPU readback to PNG and EXR, 360° panoramas, mirror and portal views.

Authoring

A cameras window in Studio, skills and MCP tools for agents.

What it lets you do

  • Show several cameras in one frame: monitors, picture-in-picture, portraits.
  • Capture game frames to PNG — including for the website and tests.
  • Replay a prepared frame into secondary cameras without simulating again.
  • Split the screen between players or cameras, each with its own post-processing and visible layers.

  3. DoneNov 20, 2026

    Native validation on Metal

  4. DoneNov 20, 2026

    Scheduler, camera stack, director and split-screen

    Camera scheduling by priority and dependencies, a base camera with overlays, a director that picks views and blends between them, split-screen and picture-in-picture in one screen pass. Split-screen and a director blend are shown in the Sponza frame on Metal.

  5. DoneNov 21, 2026

    Physical lens and depth of field

    Per-camera exposure and DoF from lens parameters through PRISMA; checked on Mac (Metal).

  6. DoneNov 21, 2026

    HDR/EXR sequences, camera shake and rails

    EXR recording, spline rails and noise shake; checked on Mac (Metal).

  7. DoneNov 22, 2026

    Per-camera PostFX and layers

    Per-camera PRISMA post-processing, TAA and reflection history, exposure and layer mask. In the Sponza frame on Metal two cameras are processed differently and one does not see an object on a hidden layer. Layers filter models only for now.

  8. DoneNov 22, 2026

    Cubemaps, panoramas, mirrors and portals

    Six cube faces and a 360° panorama, mirror and portal cameras clipped at their plane — shown in the Sponza frame on Metal. All cube faces share one sun shadow map, so lighting does not break at the panorama seams; an upside-down panorama was fixed along the way.

  9. DoneNov 22, 2026

    Shared camera bodies and presets

    The map camera, the follow camera (look-ahead, speed zoom, map bounds), the chase camera and the first-person look now live in one place and behave the same in any project. A camera block picks a ready preset in one line — "preset": "follow-speed" — and overrides any keys; the ECS rig gained chase and first-person modes on the same math.

  10. DoneNov 22, 2026

    Follow camera in ECS, presets in Studio and MCP, planet orbit

    An ECS scene camera can work like the game follow camera: angle, distance, smoothing, velocity lead, speed zoom and bounds come from a follow block in cameras.json, presets included. The Studio cameras window applies a preset in one pick, and the MCP tool lens_camera_presets lists presets for agents. The planet camera became one of the system's bodies — an orbit over a sphere.

  11. Now
    In progressno date set

    Stereo and OpenXR

    Stereo pairs from one camera and eyes from XR runtime data (pose and field-of-view angles) are rendered on Metal. OpenXR is still to be connected: headset swapchain, frame submission and reprojection; nothing has run on a headset yet.

  12. In progressno date set

    Third-person camera for characters

    The boom camera with a shoulder, obstacle avoidance and a profile stack becomes a LENS body and runs in the FPS client; the camera crosshair converges on the character's ray.
