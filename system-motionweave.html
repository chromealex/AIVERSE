What is inside

Animation graphs

Clips, blend spaces, montages and root motion.

IK and motion matching

Inverse kinematics, motion matching, inertialization.

Retargeting and LOD

Retargeting between rigs, skeleton LODs, sockets and attachments.

Multithreading

Multithreaded model and presentation preparation.

Crowds

Animation LOD and GPU crowds.

Editor

ModelView and Studio: attachments, reimport from disk without losing animation.

What it lets you do

  • One animation system for RTS armies, FPS characters and cutscenes.
  • Reimport a model without breaking the animation that is playing.
  • Animate large crowds with LOD.
