Development diary: LUMA

Every stage of LUMA with screenshots, what was verified and what was not yet.

  1. Nov 22, 2026
    Donemain · d677f8a2f

    LUMA on Vulkan and Android: direct light, dynamic GI and camera-following cascades

    Luma and dynamic GI now run on Vulkan and are checked on an Android phone, and GI cascades follow the camera through large worlds.

    Luma — direct lighting for thousands of point and spot lights with geometry shadows, GGX reflections and temporal denoising — used to run on Metal only. The same computation now runs on Vulkan, so Luma runs on Android, and on Windows and Linux when Vulkan is selected.

    Dynamic GI has a `"scroll": true` mode: up to three probe cascades follow the camera in whole cells. Probes that stay keep their light, cells entering the volume are cleared and traced again — stale light from another place is never shown. The geometry around the camera is loaded as it moves. GI stays on screen while probes update.

    TerraWeave terrain tiles feed dynamic GI as soon as they are built.

    Verified
    Samsung Galaxy A05s phone (Adreno 610, Vulkan): Luma in 9 scenes (diffuse, metal, PBR, spot lights, shadows) and dynamic GI on CPU and GPU, with three cascades and with the camera moving away and back, match the CPU reference. The same scenes are checked on Metal.
    Limitations
    On Android these are test scenes, not a game: performance on weak phones has not been measured. CPU GI cascades do not scroll.

  2. Nov 22, 2026
    Donemain · 4657a4744

    Luma: area lights, particle lights and object motion

    Luma handles sphere and tube lights, takes particle lights and uses object motion in its temporal smoothing; on the way, object motion vectors started working on Metal.

    Besides point and spot lights Luma now lights with spheres and tubes (bulbs, neon tubes). A sphere lights like its centre, without blowing up when the surface is inside it; a tube lights from the point of its axis nearest the surface. Highlights widen with the source's apparent size. In the editor and over MCP a light takes type `sphere` or `tube` with a radius and a length.

    Particle lights can be handed to Luma every frame with stable per-particle keys (`fx::appendLumaFxLights`, `LumaHost::setDynamicLights`). When Luma does not draw direct light, particles keep going to the regular light system — light is never added twice.

    Luma's temporal smoothing takes the velocity of moving objects from PRISMA, so light on them does not smear. The check showed that on Metal object velocity was not computed at all: two motion-vector shaders did not compile. That is fixed: on Metal PRISMA effects now get the velocity of moving models.

    Verified
    Mac Studio (Metal): `ctest -L Lighting` — Luma in all 12 scenes, including a moving slab with motion blur, a sphere and a tube. Samsung Galaxy A05s phone (Adreno 610, Vulkan): the same 12 scenes match the reference. The CPU reference (`lumaweave_direct_reference`) checks the sphere, the tube, GPU packing and particle keys.
    Limitations
    Area lights are approximate: a tube lights from one nearest point rather than integrating along its length, and there are no rectangular lights. No host feeds particles into Luma yet: Studio has no particles and the game client does not use Luma — only the API is ready.

  3. Nov 22, 2026
    Donemain · 781b362e1

    Baked lighting on Vulkan and Android

    Baked lightmaps and light probes are checked on Vulkan and Android; on the way, reading packed vertex data on mobile GPUs was fixed — it broke both lightmaps and skeletal animation.

    Static models standing where they were baked take their light from the lightmap, moved or new objects from the probe grid. This has long worked on Metal and GL; the same is now checked on Vulkan and on an Android phone.

    The check found a bug in the Vulkan backend. Some mobile GPUs (Adreno 610 among them) have no `R8G8B8A8_USCALED` vertex format, so byte attributes were read as 0..1 instead of 0..255. Lightmap UVs pointed at one spot and skeletal animation used the wrong bones. On such devices the engine now builds a vertex-shader variant that restores the real values; devices with the format are unchanged.

    The tests of the new PRISMA effects were fixed too: their check did not recognise the "PASS" line and reported passing effects as failed.

    Verified
    New GPU test `lighting_baked_{gpu,gl,vulkan}`: a slab with a two-colour lightmap and the same slab moved (lit by probes), sun off. Mac Studio (Metal and GL) and a Samsung Galaxy A05s phone (Adreno 610, Vulkan) — passed. On the phone the lightmap UV check failed before the fix and passes after; Luma, dynamic GI and `aiverse_vulkan_tests` pass there again.
    Limitations
    This is a one-slab test scene, not baking a real map on a phone. Skeletal models on Adreno were not checked visually — the fix is verified on lightmaps and on compiling the shader variants.

  4. Nov 12, 2026
    Donemain · da9cfbbac

    GI probes: moment-based visibility instead of a plane

    Probe visibility is stored as Chebyshev moments — the dark band on the Sponza arches is gone.

    A rebake replaces only the 96 visibility texels per probe; sun, sky and point-light transport stay the same. Sponza — 10,710 probes and 10.8 M triangles — rebakes in about 18 seconds on two cores.

    An old plane-visibility cache renders bit-identically to before the change (maximum channel difference 0).

    Verified
    Cloud Linux, llvmpipe GLES 3.2: High before/after captures, HDR GPU checks 1–53 pass on Linux GL.
    Limitations
    Moments still leak slightly through thin geometry; probes inside geometry are not relocated. MSL, Metal captures and Android are unverified.

  5. Nov 12, 2026
    Donemain · 91bb89d95

    Light baking: lightmaps and probes

    The engine's own offline CPU path tracer writes lightmaps for static objects and terrain and a probe grid for everything that moves.

    Map lights are marked mixed (stay realtime, their bounce is baked) or baked (lightmap and probes only). The runtime blends baked light with the realtime sun, like mixed lighting in Unity.

    Rebakes are skipped when nothing changed: the bake stores a 64-bit hash of geometry, materials, textures, transforms, lights and settings. Available in the editor (Graphics → Light baking) and through MCP: lightbake_set, lightbake_bake, lightbake_status.
