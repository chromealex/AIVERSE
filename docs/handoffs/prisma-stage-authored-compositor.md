Use examples/prisma/camera-glow.json in the consumer camera profile. LENS CameraRenderPipeline::executeManaged binds each declared dependency's texture by index. A producer camera sets its own visibility mask and output key. PrismaCompositor::compile resolves named targets onto PrismaRenderGraph. Studio wiring, graph editing and persistence go through postfx_compositor_set. Meshes, ECS simulation and particle systems stay in their owners.
