CollisionModule staticSdf accepts a static cubic volume with origin, spacing, dimension 3..16 and dimension^3 signed distances in metres, x-fastest. CPU helper and Metal/Vulkan regular and GPU-owned allocator kernels sample trilinear distance and gradient, search up to 64 segment intervals, refine the first sampled contact with 8 bisections, apply particle radius, bounce and tangent damping. Requires initial point and queried path inside lattice coverage; no extrapolation, no guarantee of continuous collision for sub-sample features, no exact depenetration for arbitrary non-distance data. CPU preserves authored modules; GPU strict subset admits either sphere or volume, not both, and rejects scene/ground/local planes, VM, drag or unsupported life-loss/kill response. Compute fallback retains volume. Existing immutable material-source buffer keeps its first 4 lanes and appends bounded SDF metadata/samples only when enabled; response flag gates all reads; allocation accounting includes extra payload. Uniform and ParticleState layout unchanged from Stage 137. No additional RHI binding.
