## Constraints
No automatic parking/wake or scheduler integration. Policies are pure per-component catch-up; no cross-type dependencies or implicit event ordering beyond stable-ID/name order. A domain with tightly coupled component state must provide a single canonical aggregate or its own validated coupled policy; do not invent a second authority. Callbacks must be copyable and side-effect-free; caller must supply the real authoritative tick. Plain park/wake and single-type calls are retained only for legacy pause-safe consumers; do not use them to bypass required complete plan. No new Actor, Vehicle, presentation or project-specific dependency. Existing low-memory ECS structural mutation boundary remains. Complete plan validates registered components only; owners must ensure no unregistered active system or external references make parking unsafe. Store copy/audit remains O(total parked payload), bounded by existing archive cap, no performance claim.
