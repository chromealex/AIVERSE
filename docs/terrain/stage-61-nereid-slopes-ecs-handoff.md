## Deterministic authority and system order

1. Fixed-step NEREID ECS samples actual polygon/spline water, calculates flow and local soil erosion/deposition. Optional sediment transport moves suspended material to downstream cells; it NEVER writes ground itself.
2. One TerraWeave bank ECS system consumes the NEREID bank output and reads the post-water terrain preview, resolving saturated/loose slope materials (from native bank soil or caller override). High vertices displace actual centimetre volume to lower neighbours. Rock is immovable, bedrock is enforced, root binding strengthens the slope.
3. One final `Earthworks` transaction and one ECS canonical `TerraWeaveAuthorityResource` update produces the global soil revision plus root uprooting and grass cover records. Updated bank soil heights are synchronized, retaining water sediment/carry state. `Stage54` network terrain delta can still use **one revision per server soil transaction**.
4. Native game at tick-to-render barrier publishes dirty terrain to RHI, `SpatialWorld` collision, existing `nav::Mesh`, near Flora/Horizon, Box3D falling plant bodies and grass renderer as already documented in Stage48–56. Do not move actors into a partially published slope tile.

**Limits:** This is heightfield wet-slope relaxation, not true 3D overhang/undercut cave collapse, particle debris, rigid fracture, or a fluid solver. Mass conservation of the local heightfield slide applies to equal-area terrain lattice samples. No guarantee of a geotechnically calibrated landslide rate. Up to 4096 disjoint transfers per tick and up to 500cm per pair; next tick can trigger further slides if the affected bank continues to erode. For manually dug terrain outside water banks, soil material may be supplied through `SlopeSoilSampler`, otherwise a loam default applies.
