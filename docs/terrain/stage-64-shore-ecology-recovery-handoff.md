## Correct native workflow

1. GameModule::setup: register persistent NEREID bank soil + optional cover schema, canonical TerraWeave soil/plant/grass resource, and `registerShoreRecoveryPersistence`. If using the full native example, it does this conditionally for `enableEcologyRecovery=true`.
2. Game creates or persists `GrassPatch` aggregates with stable keys, bounds, density, seed and `PlantEnvironment` values. All units are centimetres and permille; up to 4096 patches, each potentially representing 262,144 procedural blades. Do NOT generate one ECS entity per blade.
3. NEREID fixed tick routes flood volume and water samples, applies local bank erosion and suspended sediment transport, TerraWeave commits soil/slope/root support and aggregate grass damage, flood-ground sync updates bottom heights, then optional shoreline raster revises auto-bank ECS entities.
4. At a lower fixed ecology frequency (default every 600 sim ticks), `advanceShoreEcologyEcs` samples five NEREID water points per patch and advances saved exposure, seed reserve, soil shield and `GrassPatch::environment` age/conditions. Grass covers regenerate on dry, adequately lit and moist nutrient-rich patches, with persistent time-lag and no camera dependence. `ShoreRecoveryCoverIndex` supplies non-root protection to generated bank ECS cells, so the **next** NEREID bank erosion step sees protected recovering soil. Existing tree/shrub root resistance is unchanged.
5. Canonical ECS stateRevision increments only on changed grass appearance/coverage; `NativeTerraWeaveWorldBinding` invalidates/render-updates grass/near presentation on changes. Soil height/revision is NOT changed by ecological regrowth; no additional Earthworks revision is invented merely to grow grass. Native server saves/replicates named ECS resources. Fallen trees stay fallen unless explicitly replanted by gameplay.

## Important limitations and performance

This stage recovers **grass and surface cover**, not the whole long-lived tree/shrub succession, mature forests, topsoil chemistry/compaction, broad seed dispersal or voxel geology. Existing tree age/light/root-growth inputs remain under `PlantEnvironment`/game systems; Stage65 should introduce periodic budgeted age/sunlight updates for rooted plants, competition, true canopy shade and regeneration that NEVER implicitly revives fallen/harvested trees. Five water samples per patch can be expensive at 4096 patches if scheduled frequently; default period=600 ticks, not per frame. `World staged=ecs` is transactional but in non-COW mode can deep-copy large unrelated ECS pools/resources at that ecology interval; measure and optimize after compiler QA rather than claiming negligible overhead. Surface cover from surviving grass/organic crust is modelled as a coarse shielding factor and must not be confused with anchoring root depth.
