## Authoritative design, exact APIs and expected outcomes

The underlying `RootAnchor`, `RootState` and `PlantEnvironment` are NOT duplicated: `AnchoredPlant` remains the canonical rooted plant in `TerraWeaveAuthorityResource.rooted`, while `ForestEcologyResource` records only ecological history that cannot be derived from those fields. Existing terrain `EarthworksLayer` remains canonical for planting heights and soil. Default species traits (only for game-registered species IDs) define maturity, lifespan, seed interval/spread, shade sensitivity, drought stress and flood tolerance, plus cheap crown-radius policy.

`ForestEcologyCanopyIndex` builds an X/Z bucket index in 32m cells over at most the opted-in rooted subset. Each canopy has bounded radius (<=20m) and an approximate crown top height, with shade based on neighbouring crown overlap and relative height. This is a deterministic cheap LIGHT PROXY, not photometric GI/solar ray casting. `advanceForestEcology` selects at most 256 stable species/key plants for costly light/water/death/seed calculations on a configured ECOLOGY INTERVAL (default 32 ticks). A persistent cursor and per-plant last-evaluated tick ensure new generations/rollback do not depend on camera and avoid advancing every tree each frame. Seedling locations are generated with stable `detail::mix64` hashes from species, parent identity and seed epoch; ground height is sampled from existing TerraWeave. Only viable positions with adequate moisture/light/nutrients and no close rooted plant are admitted, with fresh identity and age zero. ForestEcologyResult reports `changed`, `died` and `born` for authorized gameplay. Natural deaths remain dead; none of these procedures automatically resurrects a fallen or harvested tree.

Native ECS setup, after existing `registerTerraWeaveAuthorityResource` and before registry sealing: register `registerTerraWeaveForestEcologyPersistence(sim)`, then `addTerraWeaveForestEcologySystem(sim,"terraweave.forest_ecology",cfg,traits,budget,climate,onChanged)`. Native example `registerSystems` accepts trailing `rootedForestSpecies`, `rootedForestSettings`, `onForestEcology`; an EMPTY species vector leaves all this disabled for old games. It runs AFTER NEREID flood, soil/erosion, Stage64 grass ecology, and any authored bank updates.

At post-tick native read barrier `NativeTerraWeaveWorldBinding::reconcile(ecs,WorldScene)` copies updated rooted conditions, `setDeadTrees` and `setRecruitedPlants` into the existing TerraWeave ForestScene presentation; only registered Arbor species prototypes are valid. Tree age/light-only changes do not rebuild terrain/SpatialWorld/Box3D or unchanged grass GPU batches. NEREID base current, water volume and source of hydrology authority are untouched; rooted-plant climate reads NEREID water through existing `WaterQueryIndex`.

## Limitations / remaining functional work

This is **opt-in rooted subset simulation**, not a separate ECS entity for every procedural background tree. Index construction and sorting/staged persistent resource copies still have O(N) cost across opted-in trees EACH ECOLOGY PERIOD despite the bounded per-plant expensive evaluation. Default active budget is 65,536 rooted trees, not a claim of million-agent O(1) performance; benchmark and optimize before scaling. Newly recruited saplings use the existing near Arbor/Flora batching, but comprehensive dead/recruit silhouette parity in distant Horizon requires final visual tests/adapters. The forest's separate `removed_` harvested-tree set is a presentation concern: native harvesting must remove/flag canonical rooted plants as well, or they might keep competing for light or reproduce while visually hidden. Natural death currently hides the tree, but does NOT automatically create a physically fallen deadwood Box3D body, transfer nutrients from rot, remove unrelated authored navigation blockers or modify water level. The game must consume mortality/birth outcomes on a post-ECS barrier to make interactive resources/collision consistent. Species policies are authored per game; missing policies leave old plants untouched.
