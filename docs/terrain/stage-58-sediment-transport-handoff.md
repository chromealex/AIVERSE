## Ownership, persistence and runtime use

A native game creates/persists `WaterBodyComponent` (with optional `WaterGeometryComponent`), then at a structural-write barrier calls `examples::terraweave_native::refreshDetectedShoreline(ecs,cfg,ShorelineRasterSettings{...},soilAt?,rootBindingAt?)` after water geometry/soil topology changes. The method uses a read-only `WaterQueryIndex` and canonical Earthworks heights, then creates or updates at most 16,384 auto-tagged bank entities with stable (X,Z) grid ownership. Manually authored bank entities stay authoritative. NEREID samples their water-side probes during simulation; the sediment system transports suspended quantities; the TerraWeave bank commit is the ONLY writer of changed Earthworks heights and derived vegetation. The output is persisted through `PersistentEcsRegistry` alongside native water bodies/soil and TerraWeave ECS resource; an ECS rollback also restores transport carry and bank soil values.

**Important:** Generated bank ECS geometry refresh is an explicit structural operation rather than a per-frame implicit ECS mutation. Do not register the transport ECS stage twice. The mass conservation guarantee is for the set of active transport cells; dormant shoreline cells retain their sediment in saved ECS data and do not silently discard it. Substrate type, plant root reinforcement, PHYSIS wave energy and sandbank growth are still host-configurable.

## Not delivered / testing

- Transport currently follows dominant X/Z cardinal direction **within the same water body** and **between bank-labelled cells only**. It does not yet move sediment through broad interior water, across linked confluence/cascade water-body boundaries, or export it to an external reservoir. Stage59 should add explicit wet-bed carrier cells and confluence links for deltas/meanders without violating conservation.
- Native game still must configure auto shoreline refresh at map start/soil-water topology changes and provide a real painted soil/root strength map. No automatic RTS/FPS hardwired client install is claimed.
