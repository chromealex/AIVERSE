## Sequence / effects

1. A completed NEREID ECS water tick supplies bank erosions, sediment transport and (Stage60–61) combined one-revision Earthworks slope-failure plans.
2. Native authoritative game consumes `PlantFallEvent` and `SlopeFailurePlan` after the ECS tick on the Box3D owner thread. Trees become dynamic Box3D capsules; real slope slides may spawn bounded dynamic debris blocks (independent of the persistent soil mass).
3. Box3D scene advances exactly once at the native server physics rate. At its completed-step barrier, capture fallen trunks/debris into bounded water obstruction records. Publish generated components at an ECS structural barrier BEFORE the next NEREID water sampling tick; manually authored obstacles stay independent.
4. NEREID samples actual water currents (existing river spline/field/vortices/confluences/cascades), THEN applies nearby water obstruction slowdown/bending, THEN its usual bank erosion/sediment/buoyancy uses that sampled water. Thus fallen trees and debris can change downstream erosion/sediment on subsequent ticks. The WaterScene receives a mapped immutable copy for whitewater and ripple drift. No water surface level/volume or topology is edited by the obstruction.
5. Server owns all float Box3D motion and controls replication of poses/obstruction snapshots. Clients do NOT run their own Box3D to alter authoritative NEREID ECS. Existing terrain save/rollback is canonical; derived physical objects must be recreated/restored by the server, then recaptured for the next water tick.

## Limits and actual validation

This is a **bounded local flow response**, not hydrodynamic pressure/volume conservation, floodwater height changes, physically perfect turbulence or global river rerouting. The visual water flow deflection affects whitewater/ripple drift where those effects are used, not an expensive 3D flow solve. Water sampling visits at most 256 active relevant obstructions (can be optimized with a spatial index later). Tree blocking uses an approximate circular footprint of a rotated capsule, debris blocking uses bounded Box3D boxes. Gameplay must map ECS water body to scene water stable IDs, choose real species-specific physics profiles and ensure that generated obstructions have already been replicated to clients before their ECS sample tick. The native integration remains opt-in; no RTS/FPS game is automatically configured.
