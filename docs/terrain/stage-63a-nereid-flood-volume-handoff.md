At most 16,384 explicitly pinned cells with stable (x,z), ground centimetres, up to 100,000cm local water column, originating NEREID water-body Entity, current from transfers, revision/groundRevision/tick. Each completed tick transfers a bounded amount only from a higher water hydraulic head to a lower adjacent X/Z cell, limited by gradient, source volume, destination capacity, rate and opt-in edge permeability. Incoming/outgoing are staged simultaneously to avoid double-spending water. The exact sum of per-cell water columns is checked after each tick; units are centimetre column-equivalent over identical square cells. Transfers into previously dry cells are tagged `overtopped`. A fixed-point NEREID water obstruction at an edge can slow/stop that flux. An explicit floodwater sample extends the EXISTING NEREID `WaterSample` to wet cells beyond the authored water footprint; if the original water body surface is already higher, the original sample wins. It does NOT delete/change WaterBodyComponent or its original geometry.

## Model / limitations

This is a conservative **local, finite grid flood policy**, NOT a full 3D free-surface/Navier–Stokes solver or an implicit infinite river discharge model. Water is conserved strictly *within the finite explicit flood reservoir*. Baseline authored WaterBodyComponent has a static surface/footprint and remains as legacy/background geometry; coupling total mass back to those infinite/static bodies, pumps, rainfall, streaming window migration, ocean boundaries and energy/momentum conservation are not implemented. Water can pile up behind a low-permeability edge and enter an adjacent dry cell if its hydraulic head exceeds that cell's ground elevation. Ground raises/lowers beneath the same water column without creating/destroying its mass. Source body identity is preserved through dry overflow; direct mixing between unrelated water bodies is deliberately rejected. The user must supply an equal-area cell window around the relevant river/bank/obstruction and the native app must manage a linked WaterBody lifetime.

There is no native graphics water-patch upload from this grid yet; `WorldScene` still renders normal authored NEREID water surfaces and its whitewater/ripples. The remaining Stage63 work should publish a bounded flooded-surface visual mesh and wire the native example. No natural force-inflow/outflow at boundaries is simulated: water does NOT appear at an arbitrary new river segment unless it was seeded/transported there.
