Native/host integration#

Include <aiverselib/mechanics/Configured.h>, the needed policy header and genre/Persistence.h. Executable hosts link AIVERSE::mechanics; registered OBJECT modules call aiverse_native_use_mechanics(aiverse_native_<id>) after aiverse_native_module(...). The helper puts collision backend dependencies on the owning aiverse_game target, avoiding object-library linkage cycles. For header-only policies without projectile collision, AIVERSE::gameplay_native is sufficient.

See examples/native/libraries/GenreConsumer.cpp for validated settings and NativeState storage. The library_demo native module now initializes these records in setup (module VERSION 7: adds the stage 1-4 genre records, including puzzles, cards/tactics, vehicles/sports and settlement/survival). tests/libraries/genre_tests.cpp runs trajectories, combos, world collision, round transitions, choice transactions and restoration checks. No engine source edit is required in a consuming project.

External input must arrive as validated Commands. Use these helpers inside the owning authoritative system/handler, not from presentation. Keep immutable definitions separately from dynamic state. Use integer ticks and Fixed dt. Save every outcome-affecting state and do not update the same controller twice per tick. Motion, melee and match policies reject repeated/backwards ticks. Recoil/projectile steppers are per-step primitives; their owner is responsible for exactly one update per simulation tick.

Configuration#

Scalar defaults are in libraries/spec/gameplay.json (configuration package 0.3.3): sections locomotion, melee, projectile, matchRules. Run generate_gameplay.py after spec changes. Load through readGameplaySettings, then motionConfig, locomotionModeConfig, attackConfig, projectileConfig, matchConfig. The locomotion mode keys are optional (maxSlopeMilliDegrees, ledge*, ladder*, swim*, buoyancyMilli, climb*, mantle*, vault*); relations such as swimExit <= swimEnter <= swimFloat <= swimDive and mantleMin <= vaultMax <= mantleMax are rejected with readable errors. Metres/seconds use integer milli-units in JSON; C++ converts directly to Fixed. Tick durations are literal ticks at the project's tick rate. Existing settings remain optional.

examples/libraries/mechanics/settings.json configures a double-jump controller, attack timing, a ricocheting projectile and best-of rounds. Attack definitions are typed AttackSet entries: set stable positive IDs, allowed combo IDs and cancelFrom explicitly. Graph validation rejects unknown combo edges. attackConfig(settings,id) cancels into combos from recovery start by default. RecoilConfig is a typed public settings struct; angles use turns and recovery is per simulation step.

Narrative JSON uses readStory and the example examples/libraries/mechanics/story.json. Every node and choice has a localization key. Choice next:0 ends the graph; conditions reference integer fact IDs. Missing facts fail their conditions. Define an explicit ending choice even for a final dialogue node. Unknown fields, duplicate IDs, broken edges and invalid integer values are rejected during loading.

Locomotion modes#

genre/LocomotionModes.h adds LocomotionModeConfig/State/Input/Result and stepLocomotion. It wraps stepMotion for Ground/Air (behaviour is bit-identical on flat ground) and adds modes driven only by LocomotionEnvironment, the per-tick report of the collision owner:

  • Slopes: ground normal with normal.y >= cos(maxSlope) is walkable and the velocity follows the plane; steeper ground slides along the downhill tangent (slideAcceleration, capped at slideSpeed), with no jump. Events SlideStart/SlideEnd.
  • Ledge: while falling (or on grab) next to a reported ledge whose top is ledgeMin..ledgeMax above the feet: hang, shimmy with wish.x along cross(up, normal), jump/up climbs (timed LedgeClimb phase), drop/down releases. A released ledge is ignored for ledgeRegrabTicks.
  • Ladder: grab attaches, vertical*ladderSpeed; ladderTop + up runs a timed exit phase onto the top, ladderBottom/grounded + down exits to Ground; jump pushes off along the ladder normal.
  • Swim: immersion >= swimEnterDepth enters (not while rising), < swimExitDepth leaves. Buoyancy drives the body to floatDepth; vertical input dives/surfaces (Dived/Surfaced at diveDepth); a jump with the head above water leaves the water at waterJumpSpeed.
  • Climb: grab on a climbable wall (climbEnabled); moves along the wall, consumes stamina per tick (built-in counter or the owner's pool through stepLocomotionWithStamina), wall-jumps, reaches a reported ledge at the top or falls with StaminaDepleted.
  • Mantle/vault: jump next to an obstacle mantleMin..mantleMax high starts a timed phase: rise for the first half, move over obstacleDepth for the rest. On the ground, obstacles up to vaultMaxHeight taken at >= vaultMinSpeed vault in vaultTicks and keep momentum. A ceiling during the rise aborts.
  • Dash (0.3.0): dashPressed dashes along wish (or facing when there is no wish) at dashSpeed for dashTicks with no gravity, then leaves at dashEndSpeed. Ground dashes are free; airborne ones use one of airDashes, refilled on landing or touching a wall move. dashCooldownTicks after the dash ends; a wall ahead (wallContact, normal against the dash) stops it with no momentum. Events DashStart/DashEnd.
  • Wall slide (0.3.0): airborne, falling, wallContact reported and wish pushing into the wall: fall speed is capped at wallSlideSpeed; jump wall-jumps (contactNormal*wallJumpPush + jumpSpeed). Stops when grounded, the contact ends or the push stops.
  • Wall run (0.3.0): airborne with wallContact and speed along the wall >= wallRunMinSpeed: runs along the wall tangent at wallRunSpeed (same direction as before), vertical speed starts at max(vy,0) and falls with gravity*wallRunGravityScale, up to wallRunTicks. Ends on ground, lost contact, drop or timeout (then wallRunCooldownTicks before the next run); jump leaves with push + run speed + jumpSpeed.
  • Grapple (0.3.0): the owner aims (raycast) and reports grappleAnchor + grappleOffset (anchor minus body) every tick; grapplePressed attaches when the offset is grappleArrive..grappleMaxLength. Pull (default) flies to the anchor at grappleSpeed and lets go within grappleArrive. Swing (grappleSwing) keeps gravity and light air control, the rope only pulls (outward radial velocity is removed when taut, stretch is corrected at up to grappleSpeed) and vertical reels at grappleReelSpeed. Jump, grappleReleased, a lost anchor or grappleTicks let go. Events GrappleAttach/GrappleRelease.
  • One-way platforms (0.3.0): the owner sets groundOneWay when standing on one; drop on it leaves the ground (DropThrough) and LocomotionResult::ignoreOneWay stays true for oneWayDropTicks so the collision sweep lets the body fall through. oneWaySupports(top, previousFeet, feet, vy, ignoreOneWay) is the owner's landing test: only from above, not while rising, not during drop-through.
  • Glide (0.3.0): airborne, falling, no wall, jumpHeld (a level, not the jump press): fall speed is capped at glideFallSpeed and the body moves at glideForwardSpeed along wish (or facing). Ends on release, ground or a wall.
  • Same wall (0.3.0): env.wallId (0 = unknown) blocks the wall the body just jumped off for wallReattachTicks and a wall whose run timed out until the body lands; env.wallRunnable=false walls only allow sliding.
  • Actor host: rules locomotion drives engine/actor characters through this machine with client prediction (docs/fps.md § Locomotion modes). The earlier separate traversal-moves package was folded in here (one owner).

All moves need their own input edge or environment report, so 0.2.0 callers behave bit-identically; the 0.2.0 configuration hash and state hash are unchanged while the new fields keep their defaults (the test pins the 0.2.0 configuration hash).

The owner keeps dt constant (phase velocities are computed at entry), calls once per tick, and moves the body with swept collision. Invalid config/state/input, dt outside (0,1] and repeated/backwards ticks return nullopt with the state untouched. All state (including the nested MotionState and mantle phase) is persisted by genre::save/load (state kind 21; 0.2.0 kind 20 records still load) and hashed by locomotionHash; bind locomotionConfigHash in the save envelope. Presentation climbing/IK (surface-locomotion.md) reads the mode and events; it does not decide them. Network prediction/rollback is the owner's concern: restore the saved record and replay the same inputs.

Runtime contracts#

Motion: jumpPressed is an input edge, not a held button. The physics owner must provide the actual collision result on the next tick. Walking off a ledge consumes the ground jump once the coyote window expires. A buffered press may fire on landing before expiry. The policy clamps terminal falling speed at 1000 units/second and accepts dt in (0,1]. It does not add extra jumps to the FPS motor automatically.

Melee: one active attack owns a sorted list of already-hit target IDs. Use stable packed entity IDs that fit positive int64 (or project-owned IDs). recordMeleeHit records only active-window contacts; call it after the authoritative spatial/team check. stepMelee calls authorization only when an attack can start; make spending atomic in that callback and issue resulting effects through the existing owner. resolveMeleeContact prioritizes invulnerability, then parry, then block, then normal hit. It does not compute another damage formula. Input buffering expires; no hit may be emitted twice for one swing.

Weapons: use sim.rng() with sampleSpread; invalid spread consumes no RNG. Sampling uses uniform integer yaw/pitch offsets, not a uniform solid-angle cone. Persist recoil if it changes authoritative aim. Projectile movement uses the world's real swept AABB collision (radius as equal half-extents), avoiding fast-projectile tunneling through boxes. One impact is handled per tick; leftover travel after bounce is discarded. Every impact is returned for the damage/effects owner to process. Lifetime counts completed steps, so lifetime=1 moves once. Projectiles leaving +/-100000 world bounds are clamped and terminated. No physics position, velocity, ammunition or damage is silently copied into the existing FPS actor state.

Matches: initialize unique positive team IDs with makeMatch. awardScore accepts nonnegative points only during live/overtime, with strictly increasing event IDs per team. Persist those IDs across rounds; never reuse them while delayed commands can arrive. Step evaluates score/clock transitions. Overtime ends on a sole leader or its limit; tied rounds may draw. Match win uses round wins. Capture points freeze while contested, decay progress by one per empty tick, and commit ownership at the threshold. The caller validates presence and awards associated score/rewards. Presence counts are not trusted input.

Narrative: available choices are stable by ID. chooseStory rechecks conditions, applies all fact changes to a copy, rejects integer overflow, commits the next node and records the command ID atomically. One explicit choice produces one transition; loops are allowed without recursive auto-execution. Only project-owned story facts are mutated. For rewards/inventory, use an existing transactional owner and an idempotent event from the committed story transition; do not treat a fact increment as a wallet.

State and verification#

genre::save/load provides version-1 Archive records for MotionState, MeleeState, RecoilState, ProjectileState, MatchState, CaptureState and StoryState. Each subsystem needs its own key; records include a state-type discriminator. Load validates limits and canonical encoding, rejects truncation/trailing data without replacing destination. Bind the exact immutable configuration and definition versions in the project save envelope. storeBytes/loadBytes reuse runtime/NativeBytes.h to bridge records to NativeState arrays. Bot persistence uses the same byte bridge.

Run catalog.py check, generate_gameplay.py --check, tests/libraries/run.py and native_consumer.py. Run root aiverse_library_genre_tests and the standalone test under ASan/UBSan. For engine wiring, add real gameplay acceptance and replay/save checks. Current tests exercise the C++ policies and real collision; they are not a claim that a complete platformer/fighting/story UI is shipped. No rendering changes in this batch. Windows, Metal presentation, mobile and web runtime paths remain unverified.

The broader proposed scope is in genre-mechanics-roadmap.md.

Header genre/CombatActions.h (+ CombatConfigured.h settings adapters, CombatData.h hitbox JSON). Shapes and hits: Fixed metres; yaw in turns around +Y (yaw 0 faces +Z, +X is right). Contacts are sorted by target ID and name the first overlapping hitbox/hurtbox by ascending IDs. Invalid data or duplicate target IDs return nullopt without touching state. Overlaps are sampled per tick (no sweep between ticks).

  • block-stun, Parried/Evaded drain nothing. Reaching 0 breaks poise (breakStunTicks); while broken further

hits add nothing. applyParryStagger stuns the parried attacker; interruptMelee cancels the swing.

Weapons and ballistics (weapon-policies 0.2.0)#

New headers compose Weapons.h without changing it: genre/Ballistics.h (penetration, explosions, aiming), genre/WeaponMods.h (ammo feeds, charge, attachments), genre/WeaponData.h (settings adapters, weapons JSON) and genre/WeaponPersistence.h (save/load, hashing). All math is Fixed/integer; invalid input returns std::nullopt (or false) without calling owner callbacks or mutating state.

Penetration. traceLayers(world, start, delta, materialOf) returns every collider crossed by the segment in (entry, collider id) order with geometric thickness (box entry/exit from two swept casts; triangles are zero-thickness sheets). Overlapping colliders are separate layers. resolvePenetration walks the layers with a MaterialTable: each layer costs resistance * thickness power (thickness at least minThickness), damage and speed are multiplied by 1 - falloff * thickness (floored at 0). It stops on unknown/impenetrable material, exhausted power, minDamage, minSpeed or maxLayers, reporting PenetrationStop and per-layer damageIn (damage for that layer's occupant) and exit values. Applying damage to occupants remains the combat owner's job.

Explosions. resolveExplosion(config, center, targets, exposure): full effect inside innerRadius, linear falloff to edgeScale at outerRadius, nothing beyond. Distance is to the target's bounding sphere (radius). Targets need unique positive IDs. The owner-supplied exposure(target) (LOS/cover, 1 visible, 0 occluded, clamped) is called once per in-range target in (distance, id) order, never for out-of-range ones, and stops at maxTargets exposed hits. Occluded targets are omitted. Results carry damage, impulse magnitude and direction (up for a coincident target), in the same order regardless of input order.

Ammo feeds. stepFeed(config, state, tick, {fire, reload, cancel}, draw) is a per-tick state machine (one call per weapon per tick, strictly increasing ticks). Kinds: Magazine (whole reload, optional longer empty reload), Belt (each shot draws from the owner's reserve), PerShell (one round per shellTicks after reloadStartTicks), Energy (no ammunition). Optional heat on every kind: overheat at heatCapacity, locked at least overheatTicks and until heat <= recoverHeat; cooling coolPerTick per elapsed tick. intervalTicks and spinupTicks (held-trigger ticks before the first shot) are optional cadence rules. draw(n) must atomically remove up to n rounds from the owner's reserve (inventory, meta wallet, ...) and return the granted count; it is only called after validation, so the step always commits. Order per tick: cooling/recovery, reload cancel/interrupt, reload start (explicit or auto on dry fire), reload progress, fire. Firing with rounds left interrupts an interruptible reload: magazine progress is lost, inserted shells stay. These records are separate from modules/fps actor ammo: never mirror one magazine in both owners.

Charge modes. stepCharge(config, state, tick, {held, cancel}) (level input, one call per tick). Release before minTicks cancels (or fires tier 0 with fireBelowMin); held ticks are capped at maxTicks; tier counts reached thresholds, fraction is min-to-max progress, scale is the tier's scale (or baseScale). autoReleaseTicks fires automatically and latches until the trigger is released.

Attachments. WeaponFrame (stats with base/min/max, allowed slots) + AttachmentSet (one slot each, add/mul modifiers). equipAttachment validates slot/ID and returns the replaced attachment; resolveStats applies base, then every Add, then every Mul (slot id ascending, authored order, sequential Fixed rounding), then clamps. Equip order never changes the result. Intermediates are clamped to +/-1e10 so no data overflows.

Aiming. solveLaunch(delta, speed, gravity) returns 0, 1 or 2 launch solutions (low/high arc, gravity along -Y as in ProjectileConfig). It solves for flight time, so the analytic trajectory passes through the target up to Fixed rounding and |velocity| approximates speed; a power-of-two scaled discriminant keeps large ranges inside Q47.16. solveIntercept(config, origin, target, velocity, ...) leads a constant-velocity target with at most maxIterations solves; converged reports consecutive flight times within tolerance seconds. found=false when the predicted point becomes unreachable.

Configuration. Generated sections penetration, explosion, ammoFeed (kind: 0 magazine, 1 belt, 2 perShell, 3 energy), charge (tiers evenly spaced from minTicks to maxTicks, scales from minScale to maxScale) and aimSolver, all milli-units/ticks and optional. Adapters: penetrationConfig, explosionConfig, feedConfig, chargeConfig, aimConfig; weaponSettingsErrors reports readable cross-field problems (ammoPerShot vs capacity, shellTicks, recoverHeat, autoRelease, tier spacing). Example settings: examples/libraries/mechanics/weapon-settings.json.

Stealth and senses (stealth-senses 0.1.0)#

Headers genre/Senses.h (policy), genre/SensesConfigured.h (settings adapters), genre/SensesData.h (disguise/hiding JSON) and genre/SensesPersistence.h (save/load, hashing). Target AIVERSE::lib_stealth_senses (depends on aiverse_engine); native OBJECT modules call aiverse_native_use_libraries(target stealth-senses). Skill: skills/gameplay/aiverse-stealth-senses. This is a generic per-agent perception policy for guards, monsters and bots; RTS fog of war stays in modules/rts (RtsVision). Long-term memory/threat ranking can compose ai-perception.

Tick. stepSenses(config, defs, state, tick, input, los, occlusion) once per authoritative tick, strictly increasing ticks; invalid config/definitions/state/input (duplicate or non-positive IDs, out-of-range multipliers 0..4000, unknown disguise/spot IDs, bad noise radius) or a repeated/backwards tick return nullopt and leave the state untouched. Input lists may arrive in any order; they are processed by ascending ID. Order inside a tick: expire markers, deliver due alerts, disguise equip/blow, hiding exits, sight, hiding entries, hearing, trail detection, marker deposits, suspicion, alert broadcasts, search, records. Observers absent from the input are frozen (no decay, no search progress); alerts addressed to them are dropped (AlertDropped). stepSensesOpen uses always-visible / unoccluded callbacks (tests, open fields).

Sight. Fixed metres; yaw in turns around +Y (yaw 0 faces +Z). Eye = position + eyeHeight. Zones: Focus (|bearing| <= halfAngle, distance <= range; strength nearMilli inside nearRange falling linearly to farMilli at range), Near (any bearing within nearRange, nearMilli) and Peripheral (|bearing| <= peripheralHalfAngle, distance <= peripheralRange, peripheralMilli); the strongest applies. Strength is then multiplied by the target's light/cover/stance/visibility and the observer's sightMilli (milli, capped at 1000). Only samples

= sight.minMilli reach los(agent, target), called in (observer id, target id) order, at most

sight.maxQueries per tick (losSkipped counts the rest). Self and same nonzero team are never sensed.

Hearing. NoiseEvent{id, source, team, position, radius, loudnessMilli}; source 0 = anonymous disturbance (capped at Searching). Strength = loudness * (radius - distance)/radius * occlusion/1000 * hearingMilli/1000. At most hearing.maxNoises events per tick are processed, loudest first then smaller ID (NoiseDropped events for the rest). occlusion(agent, noise) (milli 0..1000, clamped) runs in that noise order then listener ID, only for listeners whose unoccluded strength reaches thresholdMilli, at most hearing.maxQueries per tick. Same-team noises are ignored; hidden targets still make noise.

Trails. SenseTarget::leaveTrail deposits a marker (strength trailMilli) after this tick's detection. Markers decay linearly to zero over trail.lifetime and are removed then; beyond maxMarkers the oldest goes. Observers sense the strongest marker per owner within trail.range (scaled by distance and trailMilli,

= minMilli; ties by smaller marker ID) without LOS.

Suspicion. One AwarenessRecord per (observer, target): gain per tick = (sight*sightGain + hearing*hearingGain

  • trail*trailGain)/1000, scaled by an active disguise. Levels: >= alertAt Alert, >= searchingAt Searching,

= suspiciousAt Suspicious (1 <= suspiciousAt < searchingAt < alertAt <= maximum). Without stimulus the meter

decays by decayPerTick per tick starting decayDelay ticks after the last stimulus (exact across skipped ticks), never below searchingAt for the active search target; records at 0 are dropped. The last known position follows sight > hearing > trail; alerts update it when newer.

Alerts. A sensed transition into Alert (or any transition with alert.relay) schedules PendingAlerts to allies of the same nonzero team within alert.radius of the sender that are not already Alert about that target, delivered alert.delay ticks later (bounded by maxPending). Receipt raises the receiver's meter to receivedMilli and shares the sender's last known position and its tick.

Search. When the strongest Searching/Alert record is not sensed this tick, the observer starts one search: points = last known position, then owner SearchCandidates within candidateRadius by (distance, ID), up to maxPoints. searchGoal(state, observer) is the current point; reaching it (horizontal distance <= pointRadius, one point per tick) emits SearchPointReached. A new stimulus (or a newer shared position) re-plans and extends the deadline; seeing the target at Alert ends with SearchFound. Visiting every point or giveUpTicks without contact ends with SearchGaveUp and drops the meter to suspiciousAt.

Disguises. DisguiseDef{id, factions, zones, blownBy, scaleMilli, trespassScaleMilli}: observers of the listed teams scale gains from the wearer by scaleMilli inside the zones (empty = everywhere), by trespassScaleMilli elsewhere. Any action in blownBy blows it (DisguiseBlown) for everyone until the owner equips again (equipDisguise > 0; 0 removes).

Hiding. enterHiding succeeds within hiding.enterRange of the spot and below its capacity, otherwise HidingRefused. Observers whose LOS succeeded on the entry tick keep seeing the target (revealedTo); all other observers cannot see it (no LOS call) until their search reaches a candidate carrying that spot (HidingRevealed). exitHiding or moving beyond enterRange exits (HidingExited, extra 1 when automatic).

Outputs for bots. PerceivedTarget{observer, target, level, suspicion, lastKnown, lastKnownTick, lastSense, sensedNow, visibleNow, strengthNow, searching} sorted by (observer, target), one per record of present observers. This is the contract for genre bot policies: chase/engage on visibleNow && level==Alert, investigate lastKnown or searchGoal when Searching, idle otherwise. SenseEvent{kind, observer, target, value, extra} lists transitions in tick order. Callers convert records to ai-perception observations if they need TTL memory.

Settings. Optional senses section (configuration 0.3.3): sight* / peripheral* (milli-metres, milli-turns, milli strengths, sightQueryBudget), hearingThresholdMilli, noiseBudget, occlusionBudget, noiseMaxRadiusMilli, trail*, suspicion thresholds/gains/decay, alert*, alertRelay, search*, hideEnterRangeMilli. The generated loader rejects relation violations (senses.sightNearMilli: must not exceed sightRangeMilli, ...); sensesSettingsErrors adds strict threshold order. Example: examples/libraries/mechanics/senses-settings.json. Definitions: readSenseDefinitions (examples/libraries/mechanics/senses.json); unknown keys, duplicate IDs, empty factions, bad positions/capacities/scales throw JsonError("senses.<path>: ...").

State. SensesState (kind 48) holds observers (records + search), markers, pending alerts, disguises and hiding; ObserverState 49, SearchState 50, DisguiseState 51, HidingState 52 are also records. Kinds 48..55 are reserved for this package. Genre bots (action-bots 0.1.0)#

Headers under bots/: GenreCommon.h (difficulty, perception record, target pick), MeleeBot.h, ShooterBot.h, ObjectivePolicy.h, SquadRoles.h, GenreBotPersistence.h, umbrella GenreBots.h, settings adapters GenreBotConfig.h. Skill: skills/ai/aiverse-action-bots. Namespace aiverse::lib::bots; the policies are independent of genre labels.

Contract. Each policy is a pure decision step: one call per bot (or squad) per strictly increasing tick (repeated/backwards ticks and invalid input return nullopt with state untouched). It reads an owner-supplied snapshot and returns the same input a player produces; the owner applies it through its ordinary authorization (stepMelee/stepDodge/stepGuard/stepLockOn, stepFeed/stepRecoil, navigation, Commands). Bots never write health, poses, ammunition, cooldowns or match state, and difficulty never changes stats. Run them on the authoritative host only; replays record the resulting commands. Randomness only through the passed Random& (sim.rng() or an RNG saved with the simulation); draws happen only for chances strictly inside (0,1000) and for aim error > 0, so the stream is reproducible.

Perception. PerceivedTarget{id,team,position,velocity,yaw,visible,guarding,vulnerable,healthMilli,threat} is the minimal hand-off from the owner's senses (LOS/FOV/hearing). Only visible hostile targets are engaged (same nonzero team and the bot itself are skipped); the current lock/target is kept while visible, else nearest by distance then id. meleeThreat(attacks,meleeState,tick,reach) derives the telegraph (attack id, start, windup, active, reach) from the visible attack phase. A senses package can adapt its output to this struct without changing the policies.

Difficulty. BotDifficulty{reactionTicks,decisionTicks,aimError,misreadMilli}; presets difficultyPreset(0..2) (easy 20/8/1/40 turn/600, normal 5/4/1/200/150, hard 2/2/1/2000/0). Reaction delays acting on a newly perceived target and reading a telegraph (counted from the swing start); the decision interval re-plans movement and re-samples aim error; misreads ignore a read swing.

Melee bot. stepMeleeBot(config, attacks, dodge, guard, state, tick, self, seen, rng) -> MeleeInput. Spacing band [minRange,maxRange]: approach beyond, retreat inside, strafe within (side flips every strafeTicks; outside attackRange the strafe also closes in). Openings: when idle and within attackRange it requests opener with openMilli chance per tick (avoids mirrored trades), never into a raised guard when respectGuard unless the target is vulnerable (recovery/stagger: always punished). Combos: only after a hit confirm on this swing, requested when age + bufferTicks >= cancelFrom so stepMelee starts the follow-up at the cancel frame. Defense: a swing in reach is read once after the reaction delay; the answer is Dodge (start at activeStart - invulnFrom, so the i-frames cover the first active frames), Guard (raise at activeStart - parryTicks/2, perfect-parry window straddles the first active frame; hold until the swing ends), Ignore (misread) or Retreat (no dodge ready and unblockable). dodgeMilli biases dodge vs guard. Lock-on: toggles to acquire once reacted. Stagger/committed swings hold position.

Shooter bot. stepShooterBot(config, feed, state, tick, self, seen, cover, rng) -> ShooterInput. View turns at most turnPerTick per axis towards the solution plus seeded aim error, minus recoilCompensationMilli of the current recoil. Hitscan aims at the reported position; projectile weapons (projectileSpeed > 0) lead through genre::solveIntercept with aim (AimConfig) and do not fire when no solution exists. Fire (held trigger level) only when aligned within fireTolerance, recoil settled under recoilTolerance (burst control), in maxRange, round available and the feed Ready. Reload when the magazine cannot fire and reserve remains, or with no target below reloadIdleMilli; never while engaging with rounds left. Heat hysteresis: stop at heatStopMilli, resume at heatResumeMilli of capacity (or while Overheated). Cover: while hurt (retreatHealthMilli), reloading or cooling with a target, choose the reachable candidate with the best protectionMilli - distance*coverDistanceWeight (ties by id), keep it until the next decision, move towards it (still firing when possible). Band: approach beyond preferredRange, retreat inside minRange, strafe between. Decisions Idle/Engage/Reload/Cool/Cover are diagnostics.

Squad roles. stepSquad(config, state, tick, objective, stance, members) -> SquadPlan. Counts per role from the stance mix (offense/defense weights, largest remainder, ties by role index). Reassignment on the first call, when the alive composition (ids, capability masks, stance) changes, or every intervalTicks; otherwise stored roles and heading keep goals stable. Fill order Support, Flanker, Defender, Attacker; per slot the unassigned member with the smallest key (cannot take the role, keep: same previous role < new or surplus role < other, squared distance to the role anchor, id), so survivors keep roles after a death when counts allow. Goals: attackers in attackShape at the objective, defenders on a ring of defendRadius, support in a line supportDistance behind, flankers in a column flankDistance to the right of the heading. Orders are goals for each member's own controller/navigation.

Objective hook. chooseObjective(weights, team, from, objectives, current) -> ObjectiveChoice{id,score,stance} over owner-supplied ObjectiveInfo{id,kind(Capture/Hold/Escort/Attack/Defend),position,owner,valueMilli,progressMilli, allies,enemies,active}. Integer score: value, minus distance cost, plus neutral/enemy-owned bonus and this team's progress for points it does not hold, threatened/ownedIdle for its own points and Defend objectives, escort + progress for its own payload, contested bonus, stickiness for the current choice; ties by smaller id. Stance Defense for own points/payloads and Defend objectives. Feed ObjectiveInfo from the match/objective owner (capture state, payload progress); the policy does not evaluate any rules itself.

Settings. Optional genreBots section (configuration 0.3.3): reaction/decision ticks, aimErrorMicro (micro- turns), misread, melee band/attack range/opener/strafe/dodge preference/use flags, shooter band, projectile speed and gravity (milli), turn/fire/recoil tolerances (micro-turns), recoil compensation, reload/heat/retreat thresholds, cover distance weight, squad interval, offense/defense role weights, formation spacing/support/flank/defend radius, attackShape (0 line, 1 wedge, 2 column, 3 ring) and objective weights. Match progression (match-progression 0.1.0)#

In-match progression for hero-squad shooters, survivors/bullet-heaven runs and roguelites (reference port: Drone Fall — per-player experience, level-up recruit/perk cards, modifier packages per deck slot or player, summon circles restoring fallen robots, battle deck unlock flags from the match start payload). Headers: genre/Perks.h (definitions, stat pipeline, xp, offers), genre/XpPickups.h, genre/Summons.h, genre/BattleDeck.h, genre/PerksData.h (JSON), genre/PerksConfigured.h (settings), genre/PerksPersistence.h.

Modifier pipeline. A perk is one package of ModEffect{stat, op, value, stage, bearer}; well-known stats are perk_stat::IncomingDamage, OutgoingDamage, MoveSpeed, FireInterval, FireCount, AttackRange, MaxHealth, SplashRadius, PickupRadius, PickupXp, CritChance, CritDamage, SummonCharges, SummonCooldown, HealthRegen, games add their own ids in stats (with min/max clamps). evaluateStat(defs, progress, stat, base, slot, bearer, &steps) orders effects by stage (default Set 0, Add 1, Multiply 2; explicit 0..15), then slot-scoped before player-scoped packages, then occurrence id and effect index; Multiply is milli and truncates per step (sequential packages, not a folded product); the result is clamped; steps lists every applied effect with the value after it (explainable builds). Slot-scoped perks reach only queries for that deck slot, player-scoped perks reach every slot; a bearer (owner kind: robot/weapon/bullet/hero...) filters effects. scaleAmount applies damage multipliers with saturation; rescaleHealth keeps the health fraction when max health grows and clamps when it shrinks. Built-in GameCore stats keep their own owner: this pipeline is for native/host games and custom stats.

Stacking and selection. maxSelections (0 = unlimited) counts per player or per slot; requires / excludes look at the same slot and the player scope; units restricts slot perks to deck units; minLevel gates by the level being chosen; upgrades makes tier chains (selecting replaces one occurrence of the previous tier; loops rejected). offerable=false perks are only granted (initial abilities). grantPerk applies a perk under the same rules without xp (console, scripted rewards); clearPerks removes selected perks (optionally keeping initial ones); occurrence ids stay monotonic.

Experience and offers. xpToNext reads the per-level table (xp.toNext, by current level), past its end adds xpGrowthPerLevel or caps; without a table the linear xp.curve (progression-curves systems::curve) is used; maxLevel caps both. grantXp adds kill/objective xp; with pickupBonus the player's PickupXp stat scales it and keeps the fractional remainder. Physical interactions (physical-interactions 0.1.0)#

Headers under genre/: PhysicalInteractions.h (umbrella + InteractionDefinitions), Carry.h, Push.h, Ropes.h, Doors.h, Platforms.h, Breakables.h, shared PhysicalCommon.h (events, WorldCollision, swept move) and InteractionConfig.h, settings adapter InteractionConfigured.h, JSON InteractionData.h, records InteractionPersistence.h. Target AIVERSE::lib_physical_interactions (depends on configuration, aiverse_engine, aiverse_world); native OBJECT modules call aiverse_native_use_libraries(target physical-interactions). Skill: skills/gameplay/aiverse-physical-interactions. Separate from object-interactions (action menus and built-in engine interaction commands): a preview there never authorizes these mutations.

Determinism and network model. These policies are the authoritative gameplay state for interactions. They use Fixed math, integer ticks and ascending-ID order, call only owner-supplied pure collision queries and keep every outcome-affecting value in versioned records. Run them on the authority (server or lockstep peers) from validated Commands, once per tick each; clients predict by running the same steps and roll back by restoring the records and replaying inputs. The optional Box3D module (docs/physics.md) is not deterministic across builds/CPUs and is non-authoritative here: it may simulate debris from DebrisRequest (seeded) or cosmetic motion, but nothing is read back from it. If a game makes Box3D authoritative instead, it needs its own command/save/network integration.

Collision. Any object with sweep(start,delta,half,ignore) -> InteractionHit and overlapping(box,ignore) -> ascending ids works; WorldCollision(spatialWorld) adapts engine/world::SpatialWorld (sorted candidates, ties by smaller id). Moves use physSweep: the box shrunk by skin is swept over the move extended by skin and stops so the real box touches the contact. Touching bodies can slide or separate; pressing into a surface reports a hit without creeping; starting inside blocks. Overlap tests are strict (touching faces do not block).

Common contract. Each step validates config, definitions, state and input first; invalid data, dt outside (0,1] or a repeated/backwards tick return nullopt with the state untouched. Inputs may arrive in any order; they are processed by ascending ids. Outputs are the updated state plus InteractionEvent{kind, object, actor, value, collider, position} (value = reason code, impulse/tension in milli-units, stage id, ...) and per-mechanic status lists. No rendering, audio or animation. Units: metres, kilograms, seconds, newtons; yaw/pitch in turns (yaw 0 faces +Z).

Carry/throw. stepCarry processes flights first (ascending object), then carriers (ascending id): drop, throw when the charge level falls, pickup, hold update. Pickup is refused (PickupRefused: TooHeavy above maxMass, OutOfReach beyond reach from the hold anchor = feet + holdHeight, Busy when held/flying). The held object follows carryHoldPoint (anchor + forward*holdDistance) at most followSpeed per second with a swept move ignoring itself and the carrier; blocked and farther than breakDistance from the hold point -> DroppedBlocked (collider set). CarrierStatus::speedMilli = max(minSpeedMilli, 1000 - mass*speedPenaltyMilli) is the carrier speed multiplier for the movement owner. Throws: impulse grows linearly from throwMinImpulse to throwMaxImpulse over chargeTicks; velocity = forward*impulse/mass + carrier velocity. stepFlight (v.y -= g*dt, swept move by v*dt) runs once per tick from the tick after the throw; previewThrow runs the identical step, so points[k] equals the flight position k+1 ticks after the throw and the landing (Landed, collider/normal) matches exactly. maxFlightTicks ends with FlightExpired. Free objects are the owner's (it reports their poses for pickup); releaseCarried forgets a prop.

Push/pull. addPushable registers a body. Per object the forces of all commands add up (horizontal part only); motion needs |F| > friction*mass*g (PushResisted TooWeak on the rising edge; pulling a non-pullable object -> NotPullable). Free mode: a = (|F| - resistance)/m along F, capped at pushMaxSpeed; without enough force kinetic friction (friction*g) slows it; the swept move slides along walls (PushBlocked on the rising edge, PushStarted / PushStopped). Grid mode: one cell along the dominant axis after a swept clearance test, linear over gridMoveTicks, PushArrived exactly on the cell; commands during a move are ignored. Pushables never fall: the owner keeps them on the ground. Pull = the actor gives the direction toward itself; its own collider is not ignored, so step it back first.

Ropes. makeRope(def, a, b) creates segments+1 particles. Ends: Free, Point (world point), Body (follows the owner pose in RopeInput::bodies, keeps the last pose when missing) and Load (a hanging mass simulated by the rope). Each tick: attach/detach commands (RopeAttached/RopeDetached), gravity + damping prediction, then ropeIterations Gauss-Seidel XPBD passes over the distance constraints (stretch only, compliance in m/N, 0 = rigid). Tension = max segment force (lambda/dt^2); with a fixed iteration count a heavier load stretches the rope slightly more, and more iterations converge closer to the rest length. Above breakForce (0 = unbreakable) the rope breaks (RopeBroken, value = tension in milli-newtons) and stops simulating. RopeStatus::pullA/pullB is the force on a Body end for its own controller. dt must be >= 1/1000 s. No rope-vs-world or self collision.

Doors. addDoor from DoorDef (closed panel box, Hinged: vertical pivot + openAngle in turns, Sliding: slide offset). Opening takes openTicks, closing closeTicks (exact integer progress), autoCloseTicks after DoorOpened start closing. Each tick the next pose (conservative AABB for hinged doors) is tested with overlapping ignoring the door's collider: a blocked closing door reverses (DoorReversed, autoReverse) or holds (DoorBlocked), a blocked opening door holds. Locked doors refuse opening (DoorRefused Locked) unless the actor's keys contain key (then DoorUnlocked + opening); Lock/Unlock need the key (NoKey); one-way doors open only from oneWay side of the panel's thinner axis (WrongSide); scripted commands bypass keys and sides. Update the door collider from DoorStatus::bounds after every tick; give the door query the bodies (characters, props), not its static frame.

Moving platforms. addPlatform; constant speed along waypoints (Loop, PingPong, Once), stationary for exactly waits[i] ticks after arriving at waypoint i (PlatformArrived/PlatformDeparted, value = waypoint index); the rest of the travel in an arrival tick is discarded; PlatformCommand starts/stops. Translation only. The collision owner reports RiderContact{rider, platform} from its ground test (the same contact that sets LocomotionEnvironment:: grounded); the report lists RiderCarry{rider, platform, delta, velocity} in ascending rider id (RiderBoarded/RiderLeft). Owner order per tick: contacts -> stepPlatforms -> add delta to the rider (swept) -> stepLocomotion with env.grounded -> move by the locomotion velocity -> update the platform collider. The locomotion velocity stays relative to the platform; add velocity once on leaving for momentum inheritance. Platforms never stop for obstacles; crushing is the owner's rule.

Breakables. addBreakable; DamageInput{id, object, type, amount, source, position} in ascending (object, id). Scaled damage = amount * scaleMilli/1000 for the type (defaultScaleMilli otherwise; 0 = immune), ignored below the type's threshold, repeated/older ids and broken objects (DamageIgnored with reason). Stages (ascending ids, decreasing health) fire StageReached once when health drops to them; health 0 -> Broken. Each stage / the break emits DebrisRequest{object, stage (0 = break), kind, count, position, seed}; seeds hash (object, stage, damage id).

Settings. Optional interaction section (configuration 0.3.3): gravity, skin, carry* (mass, reach, hold distance/height, follow speed, break distance, speed penalty/minimum), throw impulses/charge, flight ticks, push speed / grid ticks, rope iterations/damping (milli-units). The loader enforces throwMin <= throwMax; interactionSettingsErrors adds interactionConfigError. Example examples/libraries/mechanics/interaction-settings.json. Definitions: readInteractionDefinitions (examples/libraries/mechanics/interactables.json), errors such as interaction.platforms[0].waitTicks: expected one value or one per waypoint.

Records (version 1): CarryState 80, PushState 81, RopeState 82, DoorState 83, PlatformState 84, BreakableState 85; kinds 80..87 are reserved for this package (narrative takes 64..71, cinematic 72..79). Cinematic gameplay (cinematic-gameplay 0.1.0)#

Headers genre/CinematicTime.h (time-scale domains), genre/CinematicQte.h (QTE judging), genre/CinematicScene.h (definitions, state, validation), genre/CinematicRunner.h (stepScene), umbrella genre/Cinematic.h, genre/CinematicData.h (scene JSON), genre/CinematicConfigured.h (settings adapters) and genre/CinematicPersistence.h (save/load, hashing). Target AIVERSE::lib_cinematic_gameplay (depends on aiverse_engine); native OBJECT modules call aiverse_native_use_libraries(target cinematic-gameplay). Skill: skills/gameplay/aiverse-cinematic-gameplay. Composes with the animation system (clips/markers are referenced by ID and reported back; docs/animation-graphs.md stays the playback owner) and with narrative-graphs (cue triggers).

Determinism model. Authoritative, saved and hashed: time-scale requests and per-domain accumulators (TimeState), timeline position, cue emission bookkeeping, wait and QTE outcomes, staging status, input locks, queued triggers and QTE options (SceneState). Host/presentation, never authoritative: audio and animation playback progress, camera, navigation, teleport execution. Host facts enter only as SceneInput stamped with an authoritative tick (LineReport, MarkerReport, ParticipantPose/arrived, QteInput), validated against the cue's emission tick and the current tick, so replays and peers see the same inputs and outcomes. Every sync point and staging move has a timeout in authoritative ticks (never domain steps), so a slow device, a missing report or a frozen domain cannot stall the simulation. Integer math only (Fixed for mark positions), strictly increasing ticks; invalid input or repeated/backwards ticks return nullopt with state untouched; stale command sequence numbers are ignored (CommandIgnored). Presentation reads SceneEvents; it never writes into either state.

Time-scale domains. TimeConfig lists domains (default world 1, player 2 with parent world, ui 3, cinematic 4; custom ids allowed, parent id < child id). Scales are TimeRatio{num,den} with 0 <= num/den <= 16 and den dividing 720720 (all denominators 1..16 are exact); values are held in 1/720720 step units and each domain keeps an integer accumulator, so a domain advances exactly floor(N*num/den) whole steps over N ticks (tested over 300000 ticks). Requests (TimeRequest{tag, domain, scale, priority, duration, blend}) are applied per domain in (priority, id) order: Override replaces the running value, Multiply scales it (rounded down in step units when not exact); a child domain is multiplied by its parent. duration is authoritative ticks from the start tick (0 = until cancelled), so a hit-stop (scale 0) always ends. stepTime order at a tick: cancel ids, cancel tags, expire, add (ids assigned in input order), evaluate, accumulate; tick gaps are integrated exactly with expiries inside the gap. DomainTick{steps, units, local} per domain; at most 256 active requests (Rejected beyond). Presets: hitStop(domain, ticks), slowMotion(domain, ratio, ticks), library timePresets used by timeScale cues (tagged with the scene id and cancelled on skip/seek/resume/end).

Scene timelines. A scene advances by the steps of its time domain (domain 0 = one step per tick). Cues (dialogue, animation, camera, sound, event, timeScale, move, inputLock, inputRelease) are sorted by (at, track, id) and emitted in that order when the position reaches at. Essential cues (events by default, always move/input) fire exactly once per scene run, also while skipping (flags 1); presentation cues fire once per pass and are recorded as skipped when skipped. Sync points and QTEs ("stops") hold the timeline at their position before cues at that position; held time is not banked. Commands (SceneCommand{seq, kind, value, bindings}): TriggerScene, TriggerCue, Pause/Unpause (all deadlines shift by paused ticks), Skip (to the next sync point, or the end), SkipScene, Seek (forward = skip semantics and requires skippable; backward re-arms stops and presentation cues), Abort, ResumeCheckpoint. Skipping teleports movers and resolves QTEs in between as Skipped without branching. Order in a tick: options, commands by seq, reports, then unless paused: start a scene triggered on an earlier tick, staging, QTE judging, sync waits, timeline advance. Call every tick: with tick gaps a released hold consumes the whole gap's steps.

Sync waits. SyncDef{id, at, timeout, checkpoint, waits}; waits: lineFinished{cue} (dialogue/animation cue reported finished), marker{cue, marker} (animation marker reported, or the clip finished), inPosition{participants} (empty = all; InPosition or Teleported). Waited cues must start before the sync point. A wait on a skipped cue is satisfied. Release when all are met or at timeout ticks (SyncReleased value 1 = timed out; play continues).

QTE. QteDef kinds: press (window [open, close] ticks after the prompt, optional perfect sub-window, early press fails), mash (count presses before close), hold (press before close, keep down hold ticks, early release fails), sequence (ordered steps, each window from the previous success). Wrong buttons are ignored unless strict. Judged on QteInput.tick in (prompt tick, current tick]. QteOptions{windowScaleMilli (difficulty), accessibleWindowMilli (extended windows, 1000 = off), mashScaleMilli, holdScaleMilli, autoSucceed} scale windows symmetrically around their centre (>= 1 tick, opening at >= 1) and are applied at the prompt; autoSucceed resolves AutoSuccess at the prompt. Outcomes (Success, Perfect, Fail, AutoSuccess, Skipped) are stored in SceneState::results and emitted as QteResolved. Branches: onSuccess must jump forward to a sync point (skip semantics), onFail may jump back to retry; failAborts aborts the scene.

Staging. MarkDef{id, position (milli-metres in JSON), toleranceMilli}, ParticipantDef{id, mark, lockInput, timeout, fallback teleport|proceed|abort}. On start, locked participants get InputLocked, participants with a mark get MoveToMark and stay Moving until a pose within tolerance or an arrived report (InPosition), or their timeout: TeleportRequested (the host teleports; counted as in position), StagingLate, or abort. move cues re-stage mid-scene. Play starts when nobody is moving. inputLocked(state, participant) / lockedEntities(state) gate player commands; every end path (finish, skip, abort) releases all locks. Trigger bindings map participants to entities (events carry both).

Determinism and network model. The controllers are kinematic, authoritative gameplay state: Fixed math, integer ticks, ascending-id order, no floating point and no physics backend in the loop. The collision owner reports contacts (VehicleContacts{grounded, groundHeight, groundNormal, grip, wheels[]}) before the step and resolves collisions after it (vehicleResolveCollision(state, position, normal, restitution) removes the approaching velocity and returns the impulse per unit mass, the input for damage rules). Wheeled (arcade-sim). Axes X/Z ground, Y up, yaw in turns (0 faces +Z, positive yaw turns right). Steering approaches steer * maxSteer at steerRate, reduced with speed (steerFalloffSpeed). Engine rpm follows the driven wheels (speed / radius * gear * finalDrive), clamped to idle..redline; torque comes from the piecewise-linear torque curve (rpm -> Nm), zero at the limiter. Gearbox: automatic upshifts at shiftUpRpm, downshifts at shiftDownRpm, braking at a standstill engages reverse (brake drives backward, throttle brakes); manual uses shiftUp/shiftDown edges down to neutral (0) and reverse (-1). Tyres: static load plus longitudinal weight transfer (cgHeight, last tick's acceleration), drive/brake demand above friction * load * grip slides along the longitudinalGrip curve (demand ratio -> grip), lateral force follows the lateralGrip curve over the slip angle (turns), both clipped by the friction circle; brakes and lateral forces never reverse a wheel's velocity within a tick. The handbrake locks the handbrake wheels and scales their lateral grip by handbrakeGrip: rear slip above driftEnter at driftMinSpeed starts a drift (DriftStarted / DriftEnded below driftExit). Yaw uses the summed tyre torque over an inertia from the wheel layout. Slopes add gravity along the ground normal; without ground the body falls (Airborne / Landed with the vertical speed in milli-m/s).

Tracked, hover, flight. Tracked: each track approaches throttle*maxSpeed +- steer*pivotSpeed (pivot turns at zero throttle), yaw rate = track difference / trackWidth, sideways sliding damped by lateralGrip. Hover: altitude hold (groundHeight + height - y) * stiffness - vy * damping while the owner reports ground below (else gravity), thrust along the heading, quadratic drag, lateral drift damped by lateralDamping, yaw rate eased by yawResponse. Flight: airspeed from thrust, quadratic drag and the pitch component of gravity; pitch/roll/yaw rates with roll limits and auto-levelling, banked turns scale with lift; lift = min(1, (airspeed/stallSpeed)^2) and missing lift accumulates a sink; below stallSpeed the plane stalls (Stalled, reduced authority, nose drops) until recoverSpeed.
