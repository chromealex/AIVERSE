    Donemain · 25d057a5195f6b6ade1a6b2c38e25a48cf9d184b

    Model shadows and additive effects

    Effect models can disable shadows. Materials support two additive modes, and particles can add their contribution after tone mapping.

    Three scenes compare enabled and disabled shadows, alpha and two additive materials, and linear versus display-space particle addition.

    Verified
    All three scenes ran in macOS Metal and WebGL. Separate Metal tests compare pixels; graph v1/v2 MCP checks cover persistence, baking and rejection without damaging saved data.
    Limitations
    Windows/Vulkan and mobile devices remain unverified. Display-space uses CPU presentation and does not feed HDR bloom or exposure.

  2. Nov 25, 2026
    Experimentalcodex/spectrafx-volumetric-scene

    Motion inside the smoke plume

    Grey billows travel up the plume with thinner gaps between them.

    In fire_smoke_v2 the volume material sets the direction of detail motion. Internal shadows and the shadow on the stage follow the same moving density pattern.

    The cast shadow softens as smoke rises above the receiving surface. Small gaps between billows produce gradual changes on the ground.

    One source supplies fuel and heat. Sparks rise from the base and follow undulating paths along the plume.

    Verified
    Captured with a stationary camera in Studio on macOS with Metal.
    Limitations
    Detail velocity is shared across the material.
  3. Nov 25, 2026
    Experimentalfeature/spectrafx-html5-data-interfaces-20261123

    Smoke trails from moving sources

    Smoke remains in the air and continues rising after its source passes.

    Choose Moving smoke trail in the SpectraFX graph editor and attach the graph to a moving entity. Its nozzle and light travel with it while previously emitted smoke dissipates.

    Verified
    Captured in the native Studio preview on macOS with Metal.
    Limitations
    Injection stops when the nozzle moves entirely outside the authored domain.

    Volumetric particles in scenes and Studio

    Volumetric particle graphs now appear in game scenes and Studio preview. Depth-aware particles join the HDR frame after geometry rendering.

    A SpectraSource places the particle graph in the scene. The same material opens in the native Studio preview; a combined gas and particle graph follows the shared scene rendering path.

    Limitations

  5. Nov 24, 2026
    Experimentalcodex/spectrafx-volumetric-scene · 6f45883aa

    Volumetric smoke and layered fire

    SpectraFX now has a fire example with a volumetric smoke plume, layered flames and sparks. The visible vertical banding is absent from the captured frame.

    The effect combines a gas volume with separately animated textured particles. Open the fire_smoke_v2 graph in Studio; PRISMA adds the glow.

    The volume supports local lights and a finer grid. Smoke trails diagonally from the source while flames and sparks remain separate layers.

    Four heat sources flicker at different rates. The hot regions expand and contract as fuel input changes, while the base stays in place.

    Dense billows shade nearby smoke. The light stage reveals a soft sun shadow that moves with the plume.

    Verified
    Shown in native Studio on macOS with Metal: a 1024×576 frame captures the volumetric smoke, fire and sparks.
    Limitations
    This is experimental: internal smoke lighting and flame shape do not yet reach the cinematic quality of the references.

    The fire stays near its source while a dense dark smoke plume rises diagonally, with occasional glowing embers inside.

  8. Nov 21, 2026
    Donefeature/site-strata-showcase · 43476d685

    SpectraFX: campfires, a portal and fireflies in two scenes

    Two scenes for the SpectraFX page: night in Sponza with campfires and a portal, and a sunset with clouds and volumetric fog. Five effects in the ordinary JSON format; particles light the scene.

    The effects use the ordinary SpectraFX format and live in tools/site/fx_scene/fx. Campfire: a flame body, upright flame tongues, embers with trails, smoke and a warm light. Portal: two counter-rotating spark rings, a glowing rim, an inward swirl and a cold light. Plus rising motes at the portal, fireflies (some with small lights) and low mist.

    The particle light module adds point lights to Renderer3D: 9 in the frame, lighting the arches, curtains and floor. PRISMA adds HDR, bloom, colour grading and a vignette; the sunset also uses the physical sky with clouds and god rays and volumetric fog (PRISMA local volumes).

    Verified
    Frames captured with the tools/site/fx_showcase.cpp host (branch feature/site-strata-showcase) on Linux, OpenGL through Mesa llvmpipe, 1920×1080. 6 s of simulation in 1/60 steps, then the frame is frozen. About 1,900 live particles, 9 particle lights. Recipe and commands: tools/site/fx_scene/README.md.
    Limitations
    CPU particle backend only, no Metal GPU particles; not captured on Metal. Sponza is the Khronos glTF sample, not a game scene. Found while capturing: PRISMA draws the physical sky over particles seen against the sky, so they vanish — the sunset camera looks down. Trails of particles that only orbit render as vertical streaks.
