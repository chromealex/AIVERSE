    In progressintegration/building-caverns-20261124 · a2119fb21

    Building styles and cave edits in the browser

    Generate a building in one of 13 styles or reshape a cave wall with a spherical stamp.

    In Building Graph, choose dimensions and a layout, inspect the openings, then download the GLB and its recipe. In Caverns, the stamp radius controls how much of the chamber wall to carve or fill.

    Verified
    An offline Chrome session generated a residence, a warehouse and a functional steampunk building, then exercised both cave stamp modes.
    Limitations
    The planetary travel and checkpoint scenario runs in a separate native example.
    Building Graph / Caverns · source
  2. Nov 22, 2026
    In progressfeature/aiv20261122_building_styles

    Building styles: a sci-fi base from a style file

    Structures in a setting come from a style file: the first style is an RTS-like sci-fi set with barracks, command centre, factory and depot.

    Each structure is one armoured hull with cut corners and leaning walls, armour plates (some in the team colour), side modules, a tower with a lit slit, gates with hazard stripes and a ramp, landing legs, thrusters and roof machinery: stacks, tanks, a radar dish.

    The look comes from engine/assets/building-styles/scifi.json: materials, palettes and structure kinds with sizes and parts. A new setting is a new style file, and the seed varies one kind. Besides the mesh a structure gives collision and points in front of its gates; aiverse_building_export writes it to .glb.

    Verified
    Frames captured with the engine's model viewer on Linux (OpenGL, high shadows): barracks, command centre and factory, seed 3.
    Limitations
    Surfaces are flat colours without textures; styles are not yet wired to maps and in-game rendering; Metal and phones not checked.
    docs/building-style.md

    A city built by an agent: roads and blocks

    Spline roads, crossings and built-up blocks — an agent assembled them with the aiverse-roads and aiverse-city-blocks skills.

    Roads are splines in the map's roadNetwork; blocks are cut from the road network polygons by a lot generator and built up by style and purpose.

    Frame captured by the engine.
