Engine systems / LUMA lighting

LUMA lighting

since 2.0.0

Lighting and global illumination

Light as an entity component, GI probes, lightmap baking with the engine's own path tracer and dynamic indirect lighting.

in development since Nov 12, 2026

System docsDevelopment diary · 5

What is inside

GI probes

Rebakeable probes with portable caches and moment-based visibility.

Baking

The engine's own CPU path tracer for lightmaps and a probe grid.

Dynamic GI

One bounce of indirect light: CPU and GPU bridges on Metal and Vulkan, up to three cascades following the camera.

Luma

Native direct lighting for thousands of lights before PRISMA tone mapping, on Metal and Vulkan.

Light as an entity

Light is an entity component; MCP tools for agents.

What it lets you do

  • Get bounce light in interiors and on open maps.
  • Bake lighting once and skip rebakes when nothing changed.
  • Move lights and geometry and see indirect light right away.
