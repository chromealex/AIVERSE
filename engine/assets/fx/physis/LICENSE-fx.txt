Files (downscaled with Lanczos, converted TGA -> PNG on 2026-11-22):
  flame_16x4.png       <- SmallFlame01-flipbooks/SmallFlame01_16x4.tga  (2048x1024 -> 1024x512, 16x4 frames)
  flame_tall_16x4.png  <- Flame02-flipbooks/Flame02_16x4.tga            (2048x1024 -> 1024x512, 16x4 frames)
  fireball_8x8.png     <- FireBall01-flipbooks/FireBall01_8x8.tga       (1024x1024 -> 512x512, 8x8 frames,
                          RGB without alpha: alpha = luminance added for additive blending)
  smoke_8x8.png        <- WispySmoke01-flipbooks/WispySmoke01_8x8.tga   (1024x1024 -> 512x512, 8x8 frames)

Processing: each frame's alpha fades to 0 near its left/right (flames 18 %, others 10 %)
and top/bottom edges so flames cut by the source frame do not show hard quad edges;
a small alpha floor (flames 0.08) removes the faint haze around the flames.
