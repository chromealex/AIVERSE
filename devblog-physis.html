  1. Nov 22, 2026
    Donemain

    PHYSIS: tornadoes

    PHYSIS now has tornadoes: defined in map data, they follow a path or wander, twist the wind, fan and snuff fire, tear up debris and deal damage.

    A tornado is an entry of the tornadoes array in the map's physis block: centre, core and outer radius, height, spin, updraft and inflow, start and lifetime, spin-up and decay. The path is a straight drift, a wander inside bounds, or waypoints, looping or once.

    The vortex changes PHYSIS wind, so everything that reads the wind feels it: forces, load on vegetation and buildings, grass, particles. It snuffs fire at the centre and fans it in the ring around. Debris is torn up at the base, carried by the wind and falls. Light game objects with NatureCarriable are lifted and carried away. Units and buildings take damage through a common environment hook, already connected in RTS.

    On screen a tornado is a dust funnel with a dust ring at the ground and flying debris, with a positional roar, strong wind in the grass and choppy water nearby. The example is the Tornado Valley map of the skirmish template.

    Verified
    Game client on a Mac (Metal), Tornado Valley map: the tornado follows its path with 9 to 24 pieces of debris in the frame. A match with one seed repeats with the same checksum; replay and restore from a save match.
    Limitations
    Only RTS games take tornado damage so far. Debris does not collide with anything. Grass bending, water and sound were not checked separately. The funnel is simple — a project can replace the effect with its own fx/physis_tornado.json.

  2. Nov 22, 2026
    In progressagent-physis-firestorm

    PHYSIS: fire whirls, waterspouts and scoured ground

    A tornado that crosses a wildfire draws the fire in and becomes a fire whirl; over water it weakens and is quenched, a waterspout forms on a lake, and the vortex leaves a scoured track on land.

    The tornado takes fire from the PHYSIS cells under its core and ring and stores it as heat. The bigger and stronger the fire, the higher the vortex's fireness, from 0 to 1; a small fire gives a weak effect, and without fire the fireness fades. A fire whirl no longer snuffs fire at its centre: it sets grass alight on its path, throws embers that ignite where they land and burns units and buildings in its ring.

    Water under the tornado comes from the game. A land tornado over a river or lake loses strength and breaks up if it stays over water; its fire goes out. A tornado born over water becomes a waterspout: weaker, with a column of spray, splashes at the base and rings spreading on the water, and it breaks up within seconds after reaching the shore.

    On land the core strips the soil, carries it and drops it nearby. The depth depends on the vortex strength, and the track stays on the ground as a dark band. Everything is set in the tornado data (fire, surface, erosion), and fires in the ignitions list. The example is the Firestorm Valley map of the skirmish template.

    Verified
    Game client on a Mac (Metal), Firestorm Valley map: a dust tornado enters the fire and becomes a fire whirl (fireness up to 0.9), its fire nearly goes out over the river, a waterspout with rings on the water runs on the lake, and a scoured track shows on land. A match with one seed repeats with the same checksum; replay of a recording matches.
    Limitations
    Scouring does not lower the terrain mesh or change passability yet: it is PHYSIS data and a ground mark. Water rings are particles, not water surface waves. Fire burns on coarse PHYSIS cells. The flame roar was not listened to.

  3. Nov 22, 2026
    In progressagent-physis-firestorm

    PHYSIS: realistic fire on the ground and in fire whirls

    PHYSIS wildfires and fire whirls are now drawn with animated textures of real flame instead of blurry orange blobs.

    Burning grass is now made of small flame licks from an animated flipbook. They stand on the ground, glow additively without burning out to white, with a soft glow and fire light below them, and smoke comes as a separate translucent flipbook.

    In a fire whirl the flame tongues are stretched along the spiral, with a ring of fire at the base. The core glows and lights its surroundings, sparks fly out, and dark smoke covers the top of the vortex.

    The textures come from Unity's free VFX flipbook set under CC0 and live in the engine assets, so PHYSIS fire looks like this in every game. A project can replace any of the effects with its own.

    Verified
    Game client on a Mac (Metal), Firestorm Valley map: ground fire close-up, the fire whirl and a wide view before and after the texture change. The simulation is unchanged: checksums are the same.
    Limitations
    Fire still burns on coarse PHYSIS cells. The textures are downscaled to 1024 pixels, so flipbook frames look soft in extreme close-ups.
