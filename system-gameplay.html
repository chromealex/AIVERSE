Engine systems / Genres and mechanics

Genres and mechanics

Mechanics for any genre

Deterministic mechanics packages any project plugs in: character movement, combat, weapons, items, vehicles, cards, puzzles, colonies and survival.

System docsDevelopment diary · 4

What is inside

Character movement

Slopes, ledges, ladders, swimming, climbing, mantling; dash, wall run and slide, grapple, glide.

Combat and weapons

Frame-keyed hitboxes, parry, lock-on, spread, recoil, ricochets, penetration, explosions.

Items and progression

Inventory, loot, affixes and sockets, perks, levels, a quest journal.

Vehicles and races

Wheeled, tracked, air and water vehicles, seats and damage, race sessions and bots.

Colonies and survival

Colonist needs and schedules, production and networks, body temperature, wounds, ecology and taming.

Cards, turns and puzzles

Card rules with a stack, tactical grids, bot move search, match-3, Sokoban and solvers.

What it lets you do

  • Build any genre's mechanics from ready packages instead of own code.
  • Turn the new moves on in an FPS with one rules "locomotion" block.
  • Save and replay matches bit for bit: everything runs on fixed point and ticks.
