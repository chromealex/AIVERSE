Engine systems / ECS and simulation

ECS and simulation

Bit-identical on every platform

A game is made of components and systems. The simulation runs on fixed-point with its own random generator and snapshots for rollback.

Development article

What is inside

Fixed-point

No floats in game rules: a match on an iPhone and an Android phone gives the same checksum.

Snapshots and rollback

A world snapshot takes microseconds: rollback, replays and saves are built on it.

Scene hierarchy

Parents, local and world transforms; only changed branches are updated.

Custom components

Components from a game's C++ module register with a schema and a version and go into saves.

Cross-machine checks

CI compares matches on Linux x64/ARM64, Windows and macOS on every push.

What it lets you do

  • Assemble mechanics from ready systems instead of writing them per game.
  • Give every player and the replay the same match outcome.
  • Save and load a match at any moment.
