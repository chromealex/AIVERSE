Engine systems / Roads and the city

Roads and the city

Spline roads and living streets

A network of Bezier roads with junctions, bridges and tunnels, and on it cars, traffic lights and pedestrians.

Development articleDevelopment diary · 1

What is inside

Spline roads

Road types with lanes, curbs and sidewalks; junctions built from whatever meets at a node.

Bridges and tunnels

Spline point heights produce bridges and tunnels; branches off chosen lanes; bus stop bays.

Look

Wet asphalt and puddles, wear, markings, signs and traffic lights.

Traffic

Cars keep their lanes, turn at junctions and stop at red lights; buses run their lines.

Citizens

Homes, jobs and shifts, pedestrians at crossings, services for incidents, gangs.

Roads window

Draw the network right in the editor scene or over MCP.

What it lets you do

  • Build a city with junctions and bridges in an evening.
  • Fill the streets with traffic and pedestrians from data alone.
  • Let an agent lay out the blocks with the roads and city-block skills.
