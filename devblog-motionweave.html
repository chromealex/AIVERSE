Development diary: MotionWeave

Every stage of MotionWeave with screenshots, what was verified and what was not yet.

  1. Nov 23, 2026
    In progressfeature/motionweave-walk-generator-currentd852d1c9a

    Ada walk: weight shift and softer step

    Ada’s walk now shifts weight sideways, moves the swing foot in a softer arc, and adds delayed arm swing with slight elbow bend.

    Pelvis movement is measured against leg length, so its amplitude survives changes in imported model scale. During a step the pelvis shifts toward the supporting leg; shoulders and arms follow with a slight delay, while the torso turns and tilts subtly.

    The swing foot follows a lower, softer arc. Two-bone IK continues to hold the stance ankle, and Ada’s separate model parts follow the main skeleton.

    Verified
    Verified on Ada in the native macOS Metal preview: 48 root-motion walking frames were captured.
    Limitations
    The motion remains procedural; precise shoe-sole roll, uneven terrain and automatic tuning for different footwear are not yet implemented.
    MotionWeave handoff
  2. Nov 23, 2026
    In progressfeature/motionweave-walk-generator-current6956a70ad

    Motion capture walk on Ada

    Ada’s preview now uses a CMU walking recording: leg, pelvis and torso motion is retargeted to her skeleton with root travel.

    MotionWeave reads BVH motion and maps its joints onto a character rig. Travel is scaled by leg length; pelvis height and the supporting shoe are adjusted for a flat floor. Ada’s separate model parts follow the main body pose.

    The preview uses motion 02_01 from the CMU Graphics Lab database. Shoulder and elbow tracks are adapted to Ada’s A-pose to avoid awkward arm rotations between the different rigs.

    Verified
    Shown on Ada in the native macOS Metal preview: 48 frames of character motion and scene travel were captured.
    Limitations
    The arms currently use adapted procedural tracks. Shoe contact is approximate, and the recording has not yet been turned into a seamless catalog loop.
    CMU Graphics Lab source and termsCMU 02_01 BVHMotionWeave handoff
  3. Nov 23, 2026
    In progressfeature/motionweave-walk-generator-current2c116ac7e

    Ada walk: planted stance and body motion

    The generated walk now holds an ankle during stance and moves the pelvis and upper torso with each step.

    MotionWeave builds the walk from a skeleton. During stance, an ankle target offsets character travel, and two-bone IK positions the knee. The pelvis shifts weight, the torso turns slightly, and head motion is reduced.

    Ada’s separate head and groom skeletons receive the same pelvis and torso tracks. The result is visible in the VIVID Creator diagnostic preview.

    Verified
    Shown on Ada in the native macOS preview with Metal rendering; a two-second root-motion sequence was captured.
    Limitations
    The current walk uses a flat plane and the bind-pose foot height; terrain adaptation and precise shoe-sole contact remain open.
    MotionWeave handoff
  4. Nov 22, 2026
    Donemain

    A battle on one animation graph

    Ninety characters, each with its own instance of one MotionWeave graph: ranks march and run, the front line fights, some have already fallen.

    One graph for everyone: a state machine (march → fight → dead), a walk/run blend, breathing on top of the motion and the attack applied to the upper body only, so the legs stand or walk meanwhile. Each character gets its own parameters and clock offset, so the ranks never move in step.

    The sword is not baked into the model: every frame it takes the hand position from the evaluated pose. The team colour goes onto the cloth through the material, with no separate model per army.

    Verified
    Captured on Linux through OpenGL: a 1920×1080 frame and a 1280×720 two-second clip. 90 characters: 63 marching, 21 fighting, 6 fallen.
    Limitations
    Not captured on Metal. The model is the simple one from the engine template; retargeting to other rigs and skeleton LODs are not shown in this frame.
