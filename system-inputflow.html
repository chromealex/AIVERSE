Engine systems / InputFlow

InputFlow

Action-based input

Keyboard, mouse, touch and gamepads become game actions through contexts, modifiers and triggers.

Development article

What is inside

Actions and contexts

One Fire action on foot, in a car and in menus; contexts with priorities.

Triggers

Press, hold, double tap and chords of several buttons.

All devices

Keyboard, pointer, wheel, touch and gamepads.

Several players

Devices belong to local players on one screen.

Rebinding

Control profiles in JSON and rebinding by the player.

What it lets you do

  • Support gamepads and touch without rewriting the game.
  • Let the player rebind buttons.
  • Play two on one screen.
