Engine systems / Voxel worlds

Voxel worlds

Blocks you can dig and build

A mutable world of 16×16×16 chunks: light, meshes, generation, fluids, mobs and network streaming.

Development article

What is inside

World

Block chunks, regions on disk, ray and box queries.

Light

Sky and block light, updated after every edit.

Generation

Terrain and blocks from a seed and worldgen.json settings.

Fluids and growth

Spreading fluids, falling blocks, growing plants.

Mobs

Pathfinding over blocks and spawning.

Network

Streaming chunks and edits to clients.

What it lets you do

  • Make a Minecraft-like game on the ready module and the voxel_sandbox template.
  • Dig and build in a networked game.
  • Save big mutable worlds by region.
