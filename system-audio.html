Engine systems / Audio

Audio

3D sound and adaptive music

A mixer, sound banks from data, synthesis and layered music. Sounds are tied to game events.

System docs

What is inside

3D sound

Attenuation, panning, air absorption and reverb by distance, far variants of a sound.

Big battles

Instance and group limits: forty archers don't make forty identical shots.

Music

Playlists, layers by combat intensity, transitions on beats and bars, stingers, ducking under voice.

Synthesis

Sounds from parameters: wave, frequency, envelope, filter.

Compression at build time

ADPCM for effects, Vorbis for music, profiles for phones, web and PC.

What it lets you do

  • Give a game sound with one audio.json file.
  • Make music that builds up with the battle.
  • Shrink the build with per-platform compression.
