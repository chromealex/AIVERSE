3.0.0Nov 22, 2026latest9e7e3b807

One engine for any game and ten flagship systems

The genre split is gone, and flagship systems arrive for worlds, light, water, cameras, people and effects. Breaking changes: an agent-link token, new package paths, NEREID saves v2.

  • One engine for any game

    The game systems moved from the RTS module to engine/game on a single GameHost; packages and data formats no longer carry genre names. Races, battles and city games run on one host.

  • LUMA

    Dynamic GI with cascades that follow the camera, baked lighting, sphere and tube area lights, particle lights and object motion in the temporal history — on Metal, Vulkan and Android.

  • PRISMA

    Post-processing as ordinary effects: depth of field, motion blur, AO, reflections, lens effects, SMAA and FSR.

  • STRATA

    Virtualized geometry: a cluster hierarchy, pre-built geometry on disk, 60 fps on a budget Android phone.

  • TerraWeave, NEREID and planets

    Procedural terrain, biomes, forests and rocks — also on planets; water with floods, erosion, turbidity and waves, and planet seas are NEREID water bodies.

  • PHYSIS

    A living environment: wind, fire, earth and tornadoes.

  • LENS

    Cameras as scene data: physical lenses, presets, panoramas, mirrors, portals, stereo, split screen, a director and visibility layers.

  • VIVID

    Digital humans: Character Creator in Studio, a MetaHuman adapter, soft facial shadows.

  • SpectraFX and MotionWeave

    GPU particle effects with a graph editor; character animation where a whole battle runs on one animation graph.

2.1.0Nov 16, 2026082f4d631

Battle royale, voxels, an authoritative server and Vulkan

The first feature release after 2.0.0: new genre packages, a server-authoritative network model, physics characters and a Vulkan backend.

  • Battle royale

    The battle-royale package: safe zone phases, bombardment, supply drops, air insertion, standings and a lobby. Wired into the FPS module together with tiered gear, typed vehicles, generated buildings, rotated colliders, bots that navigate inside buildings and a HUD.

  • Voxel worlds

    Mutable block worlds for Minecraft-like games: 7 libraries, 8 skills and a playable sandbox with session, client, server and template.

  • Server-authoritative networking

    engine/net/replica: change versions, incremental relevance, 3D interest, delta-coded snapshots, a render clock with jitter and loss estimates. The aiverse_place sandbox shows it with physics, ticket admission, reconnect and client prediction. FPS authority snapshots are now deltas (protocol 5).

  • Physics

    A Box3D runtime Scene (handles, queries, events, JSON) and a kinematic character controller: step-up, moving and rotating ground, pushes, client prediction against bodies.

  • Navigation

    A navigation mesh and hybrid pathfinding: one portal graph with a solver per area.

  • Rendering

    An opt-in Vulkan backend for Windows, Linux and Android with a GL fallback. Atlases: convex sprite meshes, tighter packing, framed sprite sheets.

  • RTS and city

    Radar, sonar, jammers and field operations for flow-economy wars; a Total Annihilation importer and HPI unpacker; StarCraft II mechanics. City libraries: calendar, weather, vehicle effects, traffic.

  • Agent tooling

    New and extended skills (replica, place server, physics, flow war, capability audit and more): 195 in the catalog.

2.0.0Nov 16, 2026ad6025b50

RTSEngine becomes AIVERSE

The engine formerly known as RTSEngine is now AIVERSE: a C++20 engine for games of any genre, where an AI agent builds the game from data, not code. Around 990 commits since 1.0.

  • New name everywhere

    Binaries, CMake targets, bundle ids, the MCP server, environment variables and docs. Old .rtsproj projects still open.

  • Beyond RTS

    An FPS module (client and server), top-down hero, driving and vehicles, city traffic with pedestrians and buses, genre packages, encounters and progression.

  • Cities

    Spline roads with junctions, crossings, parking and bridges; spline water with straits, rivers, sea, embankments, cliffs and beaches; city block generation.

  • Rendering

    An HDR pipeline, light baking, probe GI, TAA, cloth, shader graph, terrain textures, render-to-texture, LOD.

  • Import from Unity

    Materials and ShaderGraph, animations, UI and localisation, VFX Graph, on top of the particle importer.

  • Online and meta

    Battle networking with rollback and prediction, a gRPC and protobuf client, meta service, live-ops and commerce.

  • Agent tooling

    About 190 MCP tools, skills for gameplay areas and a game wizard that turns an idea and your art into a starting project.

Verified: Linux x86-64 (GCC): aiverse_tests 272,105 checks, 0 failed; templates skirmish and mushrooms (seed 3) play to a winner. Windows: cross-compiled with MinGW-w64.
