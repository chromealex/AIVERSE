Engine systems / Shader Graph

Shader Graph

Materials and effects from nodes

Graphs for materials, post-effects and particles. One graph produces GLSL and Metal shaders, the preview runs on the real renderer.

Development article

What is inside

Three kinds of graphs

Material, post-effect and particles; a graph is embedded into a material, game.json or an effect.

103 operations

Maths, noise, textures, parameters and vertex outputs.

Safety

A project cannot inject shader source: only validated nodes.

Preview

A lit sphere, a post-processed image or a looping emitter right on the GPU.

Agents

Graphs are edited over MCP, with a starter material library.
