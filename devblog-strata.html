Every stage of STRATA with screenshots, what was verified and what was not yet.

  1. Nov 22, 2026
    In progressmain

    STRATA: 60 fps on a budget phone and half the memory

    The same 20 million-triangle spheres now run at 60 fps on GLES on a Galaxy A05s, the process takes about 440 MB instead of 730, and Vulkan frames got cheaper by rotating the image inside the engine.

    Same scene: 20 spheres of 1,002,252 triangles each, geometry only, rendering at 1200×540. The hierarchy is cooked on disk ahead of time and loads in about 1.2 s.

    The hierarchy reaches coarse levels: when simplifying a group gains almost nothing the builder retries with a larger error, and a sphere's roots are merged down to about 256 triangles. Up to 16 levels. With a 60 k triangle budget STRATA draws about 71 k triangles for 20 spheres; far spheres take a few hundred.

    Each sphere's cluster selection is cached while the camera and threshold stay put; selected indices live in an immutable buffer per selection and old ones are freed after 16 frames, so re-uploads never wait on the GPU.

    Memory. The full index stream is no longer uploaded to video memory when only compacted selections are drawn; CPU indices are kept compressed, meshlet tables are freed after staging, and content pack pages are returned to the system after copying. Process PSS: 730 → 410–455 MB, native heap: 300 → 85 MB on GLES and 123 MB on Vulkan.

    Vulkan. This phone's swapchain is portrait and the compositor rotated every frame itself. The engine now rotates the image with a compute shader before presenting (AIVERSE_VK_PRE_ROTATE=0 turns it off): 43 → 48 fps.

    The STRATA corner panel shows triangles, error threshold, draw calls, process memory, CPU indices and selection video memory.

    Verified
    Samsung Galaxy A05s (Adreno 610, 1200×540), 60 k budget: GLES 59.9 fps static and 58.4 fps in a camera fly-through, ~71 k triangles, 20 draw calls, PSS 437 MB. 120 k budget: 54 fps. Vulkan: 48 fps with in-engine rotation, 43 without. Without STRATA: 4.5 fps.
    Limitations
    Vulkan is still slower than GLES and does not draw the panel text. About 200 MB of video memory is 60-byte vertices and the original index buffer of models drawn only through STRATA; compressing them is next. About 100 MB is the .apk mapping; the effect of releasing pages on file reads is not measured yet.

  2. Nov 22, 2026
    In progressmain

    STRATA: cooked geometry on disk, a working hierarchy and a first phone measurement

    STRATA cluster hierarchies are now built ahead of time and stored next to the content: the game loads them in a fraction of a second instead of minutes. Bugs that kept STRATA from simplifying geometry were found and fixed, and an automatic triangle budget was added.

    The test scene is 20 bumpy spheres of 1,002,252 triangles each (4 different models), about 20 M triangles in total, with no terrain, sky or interface: geometry only. The camera stands next to the nearest sphere and the row recedes into the distance.

    Cooking. The aiverse_strata_cook tool builds the cluster hierarchy of every model in a scene and writes it to the geometry scene's strata.cooked folder. A file is tied to its source geometry and the builder version: if the model changes the file is ignored and the hierarchy is built as before. Loading 4 spheres takes 0.4 s on a Mac and about 1.2 s on a phone instead of roughly 6 minutes of building per sphere.

    The hierarchy. Leaf clusters were cut in file order and came out as strips with almost every vertex on a border; borders stay locked while simplifying, so the tree stalled: a sphere's coarsest level still had 743 k triangles. Clusters are now compact patches and a group's error tolerance follows its children's size. A sphere's coarsest level is 13 k triangles, and STRATA draws 1.1 M triangles in 20 draw calls instead of 19.4 M.

    Cluster selection is cheaper: errors are computed only for nodes the traversal reaches, clusters outside the view are not refined, and the index set is uploaded again only when the selection changes.

    The triangle budget (strata.triangleBudget or AIVERSE_STRATA_TRI_BUDGET) raises the error threshold while there are more triangles than the budget. A corner panel shows the STRATA route, whether data came from disk, triangles, threshold, draw calls, memory and frame time; a window on the right shows the clusters coloured by detail level.

    Every object of the scene can choose its drawing path in the editor: Auto (STRATA or ordinary LODs per model), STRATA or LOD — in the Static geometry window or the object's strata field.

    Verified
    Mac Studio (M3 Ultra, Metal): 1.1 M triangles and 20 draw calls versus 20 M without STRATA. Samsung Galaxy A05s (Adreno 610, rendering at 1200×540): no budget 16 fps on Vulkan and 20 fps on GLES; with a 250 k triangle budget 35 and 45 fps; without STRATA 4 fps.
    Limitations
    No 60 fps on this phone yet: frame time grows almost linearly with triangles, and in the measurement the builder stopped at 8 levels so STRATA could not go below about 270 k triangles for 20 spheres; the limit is now 16 levels, not yet re-measured. Vulkan is slower than GLES on this phone; the cause is not found yet. The process takes about 750 MB: indices are kept whole and vertices are 60 bytes. Disk streaming of index pages exists but was not enabled in this scene.

  3. Nov 21, 2026
    Donemain · 7d0654223

    STRATA: Nanite-style cluster visualization

    A debug view shows which clusters STRATA split the geometry into and which detail levels it selected in the frame: every cluster in its own colour.

    StrataDebugView draws the selected cluster frontier over the frame — fill, wireframe or bounds — coloured by cluster, LOD depth, geometric error or material. Presentation only: LOD selection, routing and GPU data are unchanged, and the view is off by default.

    The scene is procedural terrain (≈105 k triangles) and 20 rocks (≈82 k each), about 1.7 M source triangles in total. STRATA selected 15,280 clusters in the frame: small finest-level clusters up close, increasingly coarse ones towards the horizon.

    Verified
    Debug view in main (7d0654223). Frames captured with the tools/site/strata_showcase host (branch feature/site-strata-showcase, 4bf7232d4) on Linux, OpenGL through Mesa llvmpipe: 2 STRATA sources, 0 declined, 15,280 clusters, 1,384,090 debug triangles, 0 truncated, 1920×1080.
    Limitations
    Not captured on Metal or other GPUs. Scene triangles are Morton-ordered: with the procedural mesh's original order clusters came out as strips — worth checking on real assets. STRATA rejects Sponza (cross-material seams).
