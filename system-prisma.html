Engine systems / PRISMA post-processing

PRISMA post-processing

since 3.0.0

Post-processing and atmosphere

HDR, exposure and tone mapping, bloom, DoF, motion blur, TAA, AO and reflections, lens effects, anti-aliasing and upscaling, colour grading and screen-space atmosphere with quality profiles.

in development since Nov 18, 2026

System docsDevelopment diary · 5

What is inside

HDR pipeline

Exposure and auto exposure (including histogram metering), ACES tone mapping, sRGB.

Effects

Bloom, depth of field, motion blur, TAA, tilt shift, sun shafts, heat haze and shockwaves.

Occlusion and reflections

Screen-space ambient occlusion (AO) and reflections (SSR) from the depth buffer.

Lens

Lens flare and dirt, distortion and Panini, anamorphic streaks, film halation.

Anti-aliasing and upscaling

SMAA, FXAA, FSR 1; a route for DLSS.

Stylization

A sceneColor node in Shader Graph and presets: toon, sketch, CRT, scanlines, dithering, Kuwahara.

Colour

Colour grading, LUTs and presets: cinematic, vivid, noir, retro and more.

Camera volumes

Post-process volumes with parameter blending.

Render graph

A pass graph, a resource pool and quality profiles.

Atmosphere

Volumetric fog banks and ground mist.

What it lets you do

  • Set the mood of a frame in game.json or a map and change it through MCP.
  • Change the look when the camera enters an area — through post-process volumes.
  • Scale effects to the GPU, from weak phones to Apple Silicon.
